# Content audit: Black Ops III guides

**Date:** 7 October 2026
**Scope:** `src/content/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil.md`, `the-giant.md`, `der-eisendrache.md`, and the blocks of the BO3 tab (`src/content/eras/bo3.md`) that talk about those maps.
**Verification source:** articles of the [Call of Duty Wiki](https://callofduty.fandom.com/) (Fandom), read in their source code (wikitext) on 6 and 7 October 2026.

The guides were audited in Spanish (the original language); the English versions are translations of the corrected text. Quoted "Before"/"Now" wording below is translated from the Spanish guides.

## Method

1. Every sentence with a checkable fact (place, cost, round, location, mechanic, quest step, quote or story event) was compared with the corresponding wiki article.
2. Every claim ended up in one of these states:
   - **Verified:** matches the wiki.
   - **Corrected:** contained an error or an inaccuracy; rewritten with the wiki's information.
   - **Removed:** could not be backed by any source and was taken out.
   - **Editorial:** a tip or opinion (difficulty, "the best map to learn on", gameplay tricks). It is not a fact and is not presented as one.
3. **Completeness** was also reviewed: what someone starting each map needs to know, and whether the guide covers it.

## Results

| | Shadows of Evil | The Giant | Der Eisendrache |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Corrections applied (rows in the tables below) | 6 | 8 | 9 |
| Claims removed for lack of a source | 2 | 2 | 3 |
| Facts added for completeness | 3 | 1 | 2 |

Every other factual claim in each guide was checked and matches the wiki; the main ones are listed under "Verified without changes".

**Conclusion:** after the audit, no fact in the three guides lacks support in the wiki. Almost all the errors were nuances: locations described loosely and lore explanations that went beyond what the source says. One was plainly false: what the cable car does in Der Eisendrache. All of them are fixed in the current content.

## Verified without changes (selection of key facts)

### Shadows of Evil
- Morg City, 25 April 1944; characters and actors (Ron Perlman, Heather Graham, Neal McDonough, Jeff Goldblum).
- Opening cutscene: the four at the Black Lace, the mark on the left hand and the Shadowman's words. The four sins summarised match the transcript and the biographies.
- The Beast: pedestals with a purple flame; about 30 seconds; one use per round in co-op and up to three solo (+1 per round); shock, melee, grapple and reviving 75 % faster; gives no points and does not count for the round.
- Perks: Juggernog, Speed Cola and Double Tap appear in a random district, and the broken bottle in the Junction shows which (red, green and yellow). They are turned on by electrifying the panel as the Beast.
- The Summoning Key is in the box of the van in the starting alley: broken as the Beast and picked up in human form.
- The four rituals with their place and item (pen, belt, badge and toupee), how to get each one and the worms they give. The final ritual at the Sacred Place opens the Pack-a-Punch.
- Civil Protector: three fuses, fuse box under the city, 2000 points and emergency panels.
- Apothicon Servant: heart (first Margwa), tentacle (purple pods) and xenomatter (parasites or elementals from round 12); it cannot be upgraded.
- Margwa: three heads, only vulnerable with the mouth open, 500 points per head, one parasite per head, immune to Instakill and Nuke, appears from round 8.
- Parasites, madness elementals (with the red fog beforehand) and Keepers in the rituals.
- Shadowman: former Keeper corrupted by the dark Aether, emissary of the Apothicons, voiced by Robert Picardo, achievement for seeing him five times.
- Apothicon swords, their upgrades, the four final steps of the quest, which requires four players, the reward (golden Civil Protector) and the ending cutscene.
- Secrets: film noir mode, *Snakeskin Boots*, skipping rounds by shooting the Shadowman, and the 100 points for going prone in front of a perk.

### The Giant
- Remake of Der Riese (*World at War*, 2008), Lower Silesia, near Breslau, 13 October 1945.
- Full opening cutscene.
- Map layout: starting area with Mainframe and Pack-a-Punch, animal testing lab, garage, courtyard with the switch behind the generator, teleporters A, B and C, and the bridge that lowers with the power.
- Linking the teleporters and the gradual opening of the Pack-a-Punch door; the teleporters return you to the Mainframe.
- Hellhounds: rounds every 4 or 5, lightning and fog, the fire ones explode, Max Ammo at the end, and Fluffy's origin.
- Wunderwaffe DG-2 (3-round magazine) and monkeys in the box; Pack-a-Punch camo.
- *Paradoxical Prologue*: panel, three items with their location, Maxis's line and the Annihilator in the furnace.
- Secret perk with monkeys and the robot's laser; *Beauty of Annihilation* with three jars.

### Der Eisendrache
- Griffin Castle ("Eagle's Nest"), Austria, 5 November 1945, *Awakening*, 50 % larger than The Giant.
- The package (the Ultimis Dempsey in cryosleep) and Doctor Groph at Griffin Station.
- Undercroft (opens with the power), Wundersphere (500 points and landing pads) and rocket tests.
- Power switch in the armory, next to the Bowie Knife pillar.
- Pack-a-Punch: three locations; parts are sent from two and the machine is built at the third, then it changes place.
- Three dragons, their locations, eight zombies each, and the bow in the crypt.
- The four upgrades: effects and first step of each (weather vane, clock tower symbol, ceiling glyph and Wolf King paintings).
- Ragnarok DG-4: three parts and how to get them. Death Ray: 1000 points.
- Panzersoldat: from round 12, flamethrower, shock cannon, power core, and the part the first one drops.
- *My Brother's Keeper*: requirements, the eight steps summarised, the final fight by phases, the reward and the ending cutscene.
- Secrets: *Dead Again*, Samantha's lullaby, the disco, the skeletons and the GobbleGum from the flowerpot.

**Out of scope on purpose:** the full list of wall weapons with their prices, all GobbleGums, the complete steps of the four bow upgrades (only the first step is given), minor secret upgrades (Goddard shield, mines, Plunger) and damage figures. They are details for advanced players that would lengthen the guides without helping to understand the map.

## Limitations

- The source is a community wiki, not official Treyarch documentation. It is the most complete reference available, but it can contain its own errors. In one case (Li'l Arnie against the Margwa) two of its pages contradict each other, and the guide uses the more cautious wording.
- Release dates are the first on any platform (PS4 for Der Eisendrache). The Giant came out together with the game for season pass holders.
- Difficulty ratings (★) are an editorial judgement.

## Keeping this up to date

- `AGENTS.md` and `CONTRIBUTING.md` require checking every new fact against the wiki and linking the source in the pull request.
- The content tests prevent uncredited images, video links and spoilers leaking into the table of contents. The accuracy of the facts depends on human review: this document is the reference for the next audit.
