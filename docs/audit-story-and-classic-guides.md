## Deliberately left out or worded with care

- **Kino's teleporter cost:** the MTD article gives 500 points in its infobox and "free to use" in its text, so the guide does not state a price.
- **Who announces in Nacht der Untoten:** the infobox says Samantha, but her own article lists her as announcer from Shi No Numa on; the guide does not say.
- **Perk positions in Kino der Toten:** the article lists prices but not locations, so the guide does not place them.
- **Tips** ("first useful buy", duo tips, difficulty stars) are editorial and written as advice.
- **Black Ops 4** is outside the archive; the story page only summarises how the saga ends, inside a spoiler.
