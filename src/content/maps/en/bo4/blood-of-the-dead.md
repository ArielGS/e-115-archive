## Before you start

**Blood of the Dead** is the first Aether Saga map in *Call of Duty: Black Ops 4* (2018). It is both a **reimagining** and a **sequel** of *Mob of the Dead* (*Black Ops II*): almost the whole Alcatraz prison is back, with new areas, and with **Primis** instead of the four mobsters.

If you played *Mob of the Dead*, watch what changes:

- **Afterlife is gone.** Its role goes to the **Spectral Shield**, which powers the map's voltmeters and is behind almost every secret.
- **The Black Ops 4 perk system.** You pick four before the match and the map has one machine for each: *Brew*, *Cola*, *Soda* and *Tonic*.
- **The Golden Gate Bridge can no longer be visited**, and there are new areas: Richtofen's lab, New Industries, the Powerhouse, the Warden's House, the Catwalk and the Recreation Yard.

:::narration
Welcome back to Alcatraz. If this place feels familiar, it is because you have been here before. What you did not know is what it was for.
:::

## Where you are and who you are

It is **4 July 1941**, in **Dimension 63**… or something that looks like it. Primis reach the island looking for the **secret laboratory** Richtofen has under the prison, in the middle of their mission to "secure a better tomorrow".

:::spoiler[What happens in the intro]{level="lore" id="que-pasa-en-la-intro"}
In the lab, **another Richtofen** is waiting, impatient ("you're late!"). He hands over **three pairs of blood vials** ("two for each of them, survival depends on it") and the **Kronorium**. When the Primis Richtofen reads it again, he finds that **the pages have changed**, and talks about "our blood… MY blood!". The other Richtofen leaves through a portal.

Frozen in the cryogenic pods are **Victis**: Misty, Stuhlinger, Russman and Marlton.

Primis try to flee through another portal, but the prison's **Warden** shows up, shouts "I will be reborn!" and destroys it. They are trapped. All Richtofen manages to say is: "Mein Gott… what have I done?"
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/portal.webp" caption="The Warden, shutting the only way out."}

:::spoiler[Why there is no escape]{level="lore" id="por-que-no-se-puede-escapar"}
This is not the real Alcatraz. It is the **pocket dimension** the Warden created with the souls of the four *Mob of the Dead* mobsters, on the **Shadowman's** orders. It was a trap meant for the Primis Richtofen: his **blood** is the key to opening the Dark Aether and freeing the Apothicons.
:::

## The island

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/map.webp" caption="Map of Blood of the Dead." wide}

The route starts down in the industrial area and climbs towards the prison:

- **Richtofen's lab** (start): a long room with stairs up to **Model Industries**, a small room overlooking the bay.
- **West Grounds**: the first outdoor area. It leads to New Industries, the Catwalk and the Powerhouse.
- **New Industries**: a large factory hall with a **spinning trap**, one of the **Cerberus heads** and a possible first spot for the box. A tunnel links it to the Powerhouse; that tunnel has a **workbench** and a fast travel route to the Cafeteria.
- **Powerhouse**: the **power switch** for the lower part of the map is here, at the back on the left. The **Brew** machine is outside.
- **The Catwalk** and the **Recreation Yard**: the narrow metal path up to the prison.
- **The cell block**: *D-Street* and the **Library** (the starting area of the original *Mob of the Dead*), *Times Square*, **Michigan Avenue**, the **Cafeteria** (with the **acid trap**) and the upper floors.
- **The Warden's Office** (with the **Tonic** machine), **Eagle Plaza** and **the Warden's House**, with a fireplace.
- **The Infirmary** (with the **Soda** machine) and, upstairs, **the Roof**.
- **The Citadel Tunnels**: an underground spiral. At the bottom there is an **electric keypad** that matters more than it seems.
- **China Alley** (with the **Cola** machine, where Juggernog used to be in *Mob of the Dead*), **the Docks** and **Building 64**, with the switch for **the other half of the map**.

## The objective

:::steps
1. **Leave the lab** and open your way to the **Powerhouse**. Flip the switch at the back on the left.
2. **Cross the Catwalk.** The first time it turns into a race: zombies rush you non-stop until you cross the Recreation Yard and get into the prison. When you cross, the Warden **cuts the power**.
3. **Bring the power back.** The switch for the rest of the map is in **Building 64**, by the Docks.
4. **Build the Spectral Shield** (see "What to build") and charge it by absorbing zombie souls.
5. **Release the Pack-a-Punch.** Go up to the **Roof** and fire a spirit blast at the voltmeter where the plane used to be in *Mob of the Dead*. The Pack-a-Punch is freed and, from then on, moves between the **Roof**, the **Powerhouse** and **Building 64**.
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/pack-a-punch.webp" caption="The Pack-a-Punch, guarded by spirits."}

:::callout{type="tip"}
The **fast travel routes** (Cafeteria ↔ Powerhouse tunnel, Warden's House ↔ Showers) are your best escape. Learn them early.
:::

## Your first rounds

:::steps
1. **Rounds 1 and 2 in the lab and Model Industries.** Save points and repair windows.
2. **Pick your four perks carefully before you start.** This map punishes getting surrounded.
3. **Turn on the power early** and get ready before crossing the Catwalk: the first-crossing rush is unforgiving.
4. **Kill the Warden the first time he shows up.** He drops the **Warden's Key**, one of the shield parts.
5. **Build the shield** as soon as you have all three parts: no shield, no Pack-a-Punch.
:::

## What to build

::::grid
:::card[Spectral Shield]{img="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/spectral-shield.webp" tag="Essential"}
It blocks hits from the front (and from behind if you carry it). The trigger brings out the **Warden's Key** to absorb zombie souls; with them you fire a **spirit blast** that powers voltmeters. Aiming with it reveals things that are normally hidden.
:::

:::card[Acidgat Kit]{img="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/blundergat.webp" tag="Upgrade"}
Turns the Blundergat into the **Acidgat**. It is built at the same workbenches as the shield.
:::
::::

**Shield parts:**

- **The essence:** glows blue on a power box in **Michigan Avenue**, the **Library** or **Times Square**.
- **The broken door:** in **China Alley** or the **Citadel Tunnels**.
- **The key:** dropped by **the Warden** the first time you kill him.

The workbenches are at **the Docks**, the **cell block's third floor** and the **tunnel** between New Industries and the Powerhouse. Once per round, you can recharge the shield for free at a power box that **is sparking**.

## Enemies and bosses

:::dossier[Alcatraz zombies]{kind="enemy" threat="2" open id="zombis-de-alcatraz"}
The island's dead, covered in chains and barbed wire. They follow the usual rules: faster and tougher every round.
:::

:::dossier[Hellhounds]{kind="enemy" threat="3" codename="File 01" teaser="In the original Mob of the Dead they only showed up in Grief. Not here." id="perros-del-infierno"}
Hellhounds come in **special rounds**, in packs and straight at you. In *Mob of the Dead* they only appeared in the map's **Grief** version (*Cell Block*); here they are part of the normal match. Killing the last one drops a **Max Ammo**.
:::

::::dossier[The Warden]{kind="boss" threat="4" codename="File 02" teaser="A huge guard with a baton. Not a zombie like the others." img="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/warden-ingame.webp" id="el-alcaide"}
**Brutus**, here simply called **the Warden**. He can attack you from round 1 if you shoot him or his dogs when he cuts the power as you cross the Catwalk, and he returns when you bring the power back to the prison. After that he shows up in normal rounds.

**How to beat him:** shoot his **helmet** off (250 points) and finish him with headshots (750 points for whoever kills him). When he loses the helmet he sets off **smoke grenades**. He can **lock** machines and the box when he walks past them, and on this map the **Pack-a-Punch** too. The first time he dies he drops the **Warden's Key**.

**Who he is:** the soul of Alcatraz's original warden. According to the map's recordings, he began hearing the voices of "the dark ones", the **Shadowman** among them, and he was the one who locked the four mobsters into the *Mob of the Dead* cycle.
::::

## Special weapons

::::grid
:::card[Blundergat]{img="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/blundergat.webp" tag="Box or quest"}
The *Mob of the Dead* wonder shotgun. It comes from the box, and there is also a way to get it for free. It can be upgraded to the **Acidgat** (acid) or the **Magmagat** (fire).
:::

:::card[Kronorium]{img="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/kronorium.webp" tag="Story"}
The book that knows everything. Not a weapon, but the secret quest revolves around it.
:::
::::

:::spoiler[The axe that comes back: Hell's Retriever]{level="weapon" id="el-hacha-que-vuelve-hell-s-retriever"}
The **Hell's Retriever** is a throwing axe that returns to your hand. To get it, **feed the three Cerberus heads** on the walls (New Industries, the cell block's second floor and Eagle Plaza) by killing zombies near each one until it disappears. Then take the **fast travel route** between the Warden's House and the Showers: halfway along, the axe is waiting on a rock.

It can be upgraded to the **Hell's Redeemer** by charging it with souls in the Recreation Yard and following the trail of a ghost dog.
:::

:::spoiler[The free Blundergat]{level="weapon" id="el-blundergat-gratis"}
With the Hell's Retriever, knock down **five skulls** hidden around the map (in Eagle Plaza, in a C-D Street cell, in West Grounds, at the Docks and on the Roof). Then pick up the Blundergat from the table in the **Warden's Office**.
:::

## Secrets and the full story

:::spoiler[The secret quest: Most Escape Alive]{level="ee" id="la-mision-secreta-most-escape-alive"}
The goal: escape the island and, once again, **break the cycle**. It can be done with any number of players, but for the ending someone has to play as **Richtofen**. You need the **Spectral Shield**, the **Hell's Retriever** and the **spoon** (the first step of the knife upgrade).

1. **Release the Pack-a-Punch** from the Roof.
2. **Find the secret room** in the Warden's House: there is a scratched wall upstairs that someone very big has to break. Inside are a **red stone** and the **Warden's body**.
3. **Recover the Kronorium:** take the stone to the map in the lab. A **ghost seagull** steals the book and you will have to chase it around the island with spectral vision.
4. **The trials:** with the book in the Warden's lap, its pages give you a code. The lighthouse will light up different spots on the island, and each one holds a trial with ghosts.
5. **The trap:** with the five red orbs, go back to the Warden's body.
6. **The final fight**, next to a dark mechanism. Arrive with a freshly charged shield, upgraded weapons and all your perks.

The step-by-step, with boxes to tick, is on the **Quests** page.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending (the cutscene)]{level="ee" id="el-final-la-cinematica"}
::figure{src="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/vials.webp" caption="The blood vials: “two for each of them”."}

The spirits trapped on the island are **set free**. **Another Richtofen** appears, the one who comes from the **Great War**, wearing chainmail. He gives the **Kronorium to Nikolai** ("learn of your true calling… your soul will be needed to defeat **Doctor Monty**") and tells the Primis Richtofen that **the cycle is broken** and their "insurance policies" are no longer needed.

He takes the blood vials and the **Summoning Key**, smashes the vials and leaves through a portal with Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo. The Primis Richtofen is left behind, **bleeding to death**: "I was the nicest one… and you won't even remember."

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo4/blood-of-the-dead/ending.webp" caption="The end of the Primis Richtofen."}
:::

:::spoiler[Small secrets]{level="ee" id="secretos-pequenos"}
- **Secret song:** with the shield, fire a blast at the **keypad** at the bottom of the Citadel Tunnels and enter the most famous number in Zombies. A long version of **Where Are We Going** plays.
- **The same keypad** accepts other numbers: one summons **the Warden** (think of the number of the beast) and another gives you a **Zombie Blood** once per round.
- **The walnut:** in the lab there is a teleporter pad with a walnut on it. Use it.
- **Free Monkey Bombs:** charge the hat of the monkey statue in C-D Street with souls using your Specialist Weapon upgraded to level 2, shoot it, and collect the monkeys on the walnut pad.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

- **Split the shield errands:** one looks for the essence in the prison and the other for the door in China Alley or the Citadel.
- **Cross the Catwalk together** and with plenty of ammo the first time.
- **When the Warden appears, one takes the helmet off and the other finishes him.**
- **The fast travel routes** let you split up on purpose and regroup in seconds.
