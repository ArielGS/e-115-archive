## Before you start

**Kino der Toten** ("Cinema of the Dead") is the **first Zombies map of *Call of Duty: Black Ops*** (2010). It is a **classic** map: doors, Mystery Box, perks, power and Pack-a-Punch. There are no parts to build and no main quest, so it is a good place to learn.

It brings three new things: **Crawler Zombies**, the **Fire Pit** trap and the **Thundergun**, a wonder weapon that blasts zombies away with compressed air.

:::narration
If this is your first time in Black Ops, this is the ideal map. One building, one teleporter and a machine that upgrades your weapons. Let's take it step by step.
:::

:::callout{type="info"}
**Note:** according to the wiki, Kino der Toten was meant to be a downloadable map for *World at War*, but it was saved for *Black Ops* because its release would have landed too close to *Modern Warfare 2*. It came back remastered in *Black Ops III* (*Zombies Chronicles*), with new weapons and a Der Wunderfizz.
:::

## Where you are and who you are

It is **28 October 1963** and you are in the **Deutsches Sol Kino** ("German Sun Cinema"), a West Berlin theatre that **Group 935** turned into one of its facilities during the war.

You play as **Ultimis**, the same four from the last *World at War* maps:

Who would have thought the teleporter was capable of time travel? How many stations does this group have? Where did that little girl disappear to? Only time will tell what new questions await us in this… theatre of the damned!

:::spoiler[How they got here]{level="lore" id="como-llegaron-hasta-aqui"}
At the end of *World at War*, Ultimis go back to the Der Riese factory. Richtofen wants to use the teleporter to **reach the Moon and confront Samantha**, the little girl controlling the zombies. But the **Wunderwaffe DG-2 overloads the machine** and, instead of the Moon, it sends them **eighteen years into the future**, to this cinema. In the jump, Richtofen **loses his diary**, which the Soviets later recover.

It is the first time Ultimis travel through space and time. From here they will find a **lunar lander** that takes them to the next map: the Soviet cosmodrome of *Ascension*.
:::

## The cinema

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo1/kino-der-toten/alley.webp" caption="The alley, one of the cinema's outdoor areas." wide}

You spawn in the **lobby**. The areas, by the weapons you find in each:

- **Lobby** (starting area): the **Olympia** downstairs and the **M14** upstairs, 500 points each. The **mainframe** is here, the pad where the teleporter lands you.
- **Upper and lower halls**: the **PM63** and the **MPL** (1000 each).
- **Dressing room**: the **MP5K** (1000).
- **Foyer**: the **MP40** downstairs (1000) and the **Stakeout** upstairs (1500).
- **Alley**: the **AK74u** (1200).
- **Stage**: the **M16** (1200) and **Claymores** (1000). The **power switch** is behind it.
- **Theatre**: the **Bowie Knife** (3000).
- **Projector room**: grenades (250) and the **Pack-a-Punch**. You can only get there through the teleporter.

**Perks:** Juggernog (2500), Quick Revive (1500; 500 solo), Speed Cola (3000) and Double Tap Root Beer (2000). An update added **Mule Kick** (4000). They run on the power.

:::narration
Summing up the cinema: you start in the lobby, the power is behind the stage, and the Pack-a-Punch, in the projector room, can only be reached by teleporting.
:::

## The objective

:::steps
1. **Reach the stage** by opening doors from the lobby.
2. **Turn on the power.** The switch is **backstage, next to the M16**. Using it draws back the curtain, opens a door in the lobby, turns on the projector and powers the perks. Careful: from then on, **Crawler Zombies** start to appear.
3. **Link the teleporter.** Activate it and link it to the **mainframe in the lobby**; there is no time limit. It has to be linked again **every time** you use it.
4. **Teleport.** It drops you on a high balcony, the **projector room**, where the **Pack-a-Punch** is (5000 points per upgrade). Then it usually takes you to a **random room** and back to the lobby.
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo1/kino-der-toten/mainframe.webp" caption="The lobby mainframe: you come back here after every trip."}

:::callout{type="tip"}
The random rooms sometimes have **power-ups** waiting for you. And any zombie or dog that walks into the teleporter while it fires **dies instantly**.
:::

## Your first rounds

:::steps
1. **Rounds 1 and 2 in the lobby.** Pistol and knife to save points, and repair the windows.
2. **Buy the Olympia or the M14** if you are struggling to kill fast.
3. **Open the way to the stage.** That is your first real objective.
4. **Turn on the power and find Juggernog.**
5. **Try the box.** The **Thundergun** comes from it, along with the Ray Gun and the Monkey Bombs.
6. **Link the teleporter** between rounds, when a single zombie is left, and upgrade your weapon at the Pack-a-Punch.
:::

## What to build

:::callout{type="info"}
**Nothing.** Kino der Toten is a classic map. What it does have is a **Fire Pit** trap (1000 points): a fire on the floor that **incinerates the zombies** running through it. You get burned too if you cross it while it is lit.
:::

## Enemies and bosses

Kino der Toten has no boss. The danger comes from the special rounds and a new kind of zombie.

:::dossier[Cinema zombies]{kind="enemy" threat="1" open id="zombis-del-cine"}
The usual ones: faster and tougher every round. They come in through the boarded windows and the gaps in the building.
:::

:::dossier[Crawlers]{kind="enemy" threat="2" codename="File 01" teaser="Once the lights come on, something starts crawling along the floor." img="/images/maps/bo1/kino-der-toten/crawler.webp" id="rastreros"}
Zombies that **crawl along the floor** and are hard to see. If you kill them with bullets, they flip over, curl up and **explode**: they deal damage and release a **cloud of green gas** that disorients you and blurs your vision.

**Trick:** they do not release the gas if you finish them with the **knife**, a **wonder weapon**, a **trap** or **explosives**. They appear once you turn on the power.
:::

::::dossier[Hellhounds]{kind="enemy" threat="3" codename="File 02" teaser="Some rounds bring no zombies." img="/images/maps/bo1/kino-der-toten/hellhound.webp" id="perros-del-infierno"}
The zombie dogs from *World at War* come back in **special rounds**: they arrive in packs, fast and straight at you. Killing the last one drops a **Max Ammo**.

**Trick:** find a corridor or a corner so they can only come from one side.
::::

## Secrets and the full story

:::spoiler[The teleporter rooms]{level="lore" id="las-habitaciones-del-teletransportador"}
After the Pack-a-Punch, the teleporter can take you to one of **four rooms**: a **dentist's office**, a **conference room** and **two versions of a little girl's bedroom**, one normal and one wrecked, blood-stained and full of teddy bears.

It is **Samantha's room**, and it is the first time we see part of **the House**, in **Agartha** (the Aether). In the *Black Ops III* version, Doctor Monty mentions it in a radio: "That was where you first saw the house, wasn't it? Well, part of it anyway. Samantha's room, remember?"
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo1/kino-der-toten/samantha-room.webp" caption="One of the rooms the teleporter can take you to."}

:::spoiler[Maxis's films]{level="lore" id="las-peliculas-de-maxis"}
Those rooms can hold **film reels** (up to three per match, with the Group 935 logo). If you carry one and teleport again, you can load it into the **projector**: a piece of film plays and you hear **Ludvig Maxis**.

Maxis talks about **test subjects** who have spent days "undergoing treatment" and about "edits" to the films to break through to their subconscious. In the third reel, "subject two-six" starts following basic instructions. The hidden radios tell the ending: subject two-six died in a field test after losing control and attacking one of his handlers.

In the story, Group 935 had moved Maxis to this cinema to **create an undead army** for Germany. With all three reels loaded, a **small rocket** appears in the conference room and can be launched.
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo1/kino-der-toten/samantha-room-ruined.webp" caption="The same room, in its wrecked version."}

:::spoiler[The secret song: 115]{level="ee" id="la-cancion-secreta-115"}
Find three hidden **rocks** and press the interact button on each one:

1. Near the starting area, in a corner, inside a jar by the bottom window.
2. In the **dressing room**, on a black table near a window, behind two mannequins.
3. In the **storage room**, next to one of the windows, on a shelf.

**115**, the map's song, starts playing.
:::

:::spoiler[The hidden radios]{level="ee" id="las-radios-escondidas"}
Shoot (or throw an explosive at) these radios to hear Maxis:

- Inside the theatre's **chandelier**.
- At the top of the **tower** you can see from the alley, through the barrier next to Double Tap.

*Black Ops III* adds a third one, activated with the interact button: to the left of the **Pharo** wall buy, tucked into a corner. That one is Monty's.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

- **Split the jobs when turning on the power:** one goes to the switch while the other covers the way back to the lobby.
- **Watch out for Crawlers in narrow corridors.** If they gas you both, neither of you can see.
- **Teleport in turns** between rounds: while one upgrades a weapon, the other keeps the round alive with the last zombie.
- **The theatre is roomy**: a good place to train zombies in high rounds.
