## Before you start

Shadows of Evil is the first Black Ops III map and, for many people, the most confusing one to start with. It is big, dark and full of new mechanics that nobody explains: a monster transformation, rituals, worms, a tram and creatures unlike anything before.

The good news: **underneath, it works like any other Zombies map.** You survive, earn points, open areas, get perks and look for the Pack-a-Punch. This guide walks you through it in that order.

:::narration
If this is your first time in Morg City, do not panic. One step at a time: first where you are, then what the Beast is and, finally, how to open the Pack-a-Punch.
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/morg-city.webp" caption="Morg City: always night, always raining." wide}

## Where you are and who you are

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/overhead.webp" caption="Morg City from the rooftops." wide}

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/mark.webp" caption="The mark on the hand (here, Jessica’s)."}

**Morg City** is a fictional American city with the look of 1940s film noir: neon signs, nightclubs, crooked cops and rain. There are no soldiers or scientists here: the heroes are **four ordinary people with a shady past**.

::::grid
:::card[Nero Blackstone]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/nero.webp" tag="The Magician"}
A washed-up illusionist, drowning in debt and in his own pride.
:::

:::card[Jessica Rose]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/jessica.webp" tag="The Femme Fatale"}
A burlesque dancer one step away from movie stardom. She would do anything to get there.
:::

:::card[Jack Vincent]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/jack.webp" tag="The Detective"}
A crooked cop who takes bribes and gets rid of anyone in his way.
:::

:::card[Floyd Campbell]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/floyd.webp" tag="The Boxer"}
A fighter returning to the ring who will not leave anything to chance.
:::
::::

In the opening cutscene, the four are at the **Black Lace** club when they all pass out at once. They wake up in different parts of the city, with the streets full of the dead and a **strange mark on their left hand**. A voice guides them:

:::quote{by="The Shadowman"}
I have been watching. I have seen each and every one of your misdeeds. I know who you are… Only through me, the Shadowman, will you find your redemption.
:::

That voice is the **Shadowman**. He promises to lift the curse if they atone for their sins by doing what he says. The whole mission of the map revolves around that promise.

:::spoiler[Their sins (what the intro shows)]{level="lore" id="sus-pecados-lo-que-muestra-la-intro"}
- **Jessica** stabs a man with a pair of scissors to get rid of compromising photos of her and protect her career.
- **Jack** shoots a man in an alley to cover up a police bribery case.
- **Floyd** steps into the ring with brass knuckles hidden under his hand wraps to wreck his opponent.
- **Nero**, ruined by his wife's debts, asks his lawyer whether the life insurance is paid up… and ties her to his knife-throwing wheel.
:::

## The Beast

This is what throws people off the first time. The mark on your hand lets you **turn into the Beast**, a tentacled creature with electric powers. Without it you cannot progress on this map.

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/beast.webp" caption="The Beast: tentacles, lightning and a ticking clock."}

**How to trigger it:** look for the **pedestals with a purple flame** around the city (there is one in the starting area) and hold the interact button.

**How long it lasts:** about 30 seconds. Attacking eats into that time. You can switch back to human form early if you want.

**How often:** in co-op, **once per round** for each player. Solo, you can store up to three uses and get one back each round.

What the Beast can do:

- **Electric shock.** Stuns zombies and, above all, **powers up** fuse boxes and panels. Charge it to chain the lightning.
- **Melee.** Kills zombies in one hit and **smashes crates and doors** a normal person cannot open.
- **Grapple.** Pulls you to marked points to reach high places or cross gaps.
- **Revive.** Revives a downed teammate much faster than normal.

:::callout{type="warn"}
The Beast **gives no points** and its kills do not count towards ending the round. Use it to open the way, not to farm.
:::

:::callout{type="tip"}
**What to look for as the Beast:** electric panels next to perk machines, hanging or hidden wooden crates, protected doors and crates a normal person cannot open, and fuse boxes on walls. If something looks out of reach, it is probably meant for the Beast.
:::

## The objective

Besides surviving, the map gives you three goals, roughly in this order:

:::steps
1. **Power the perks.** There is no main power switch: each machine turns on when you shock the panel next to it as the Beast.
2. **Get the Summoning Key.** An ancient artifact you need for the rituals. It is hidden very close to where you start.
3. **Complete the four rituals** and open the **Pack-a-Punch** beneath the city.
:::

Along the way you can build a shield, call in a robot police officer, craft a wonder weapon and, if you feel brave, finish the secret quest that wraps up the story.

:::callout{type="info"}
**Perks on this map:** Quick Revive (1500, or 500 solo), Juggernog (2500), Double Tap (2000), Stamin-Up (2000), Speed Cola (3000), Mule Kick (4000) and Widow's Wine (4000). Once the Pack-a-Punch is open, upgrading a weapon again for 2500 gives it one of these effects at random: Thunder Wall, Blast Furnace, Fireworks, Turned or Dead Wire.
:::

## Your first rounds

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/train.webp" caption="Concept art of the tram that links the districts."}

:::steps
1. **Rounds 1 and 2: save up.** Kill with your pistol and knife. **Quick Revive** is next to the truck in the starting area (only 500 when playing solo).
2. **Take a good look at the truck in the starting alley.** There is a wooden crate only the Beast can smash. Pick up what is inside afterwards in human form: it is the **Summoning Key**.
3. **Grab the Fumigator.** There are several near the start. It lets you "harvest" the **pods** that grow around the map and drop points, weapons or ammo.
4. **Open the way to the Junction.** It is the central square leading to the three districts: **Canal**, **Footlight** (the theatre district) and **Waterfront** (the docks).
5. **Look at the broken bottle in the Junction.** Juggernog, Speed Cola and Double Tap spawn in a different district each match. The bottle's colour tells you where: **red** Juggernog, **green** Speed Cola, **yellow** Double Tap.
6. **Go for Juggernog.** Open that district, turn into the Beast and shock the machine's panel. Buy it before round 10.
7. **Use the tram.** It links the three districts and saves you crossing the city on foot with a horde behind you.
:::

:::narration
One tip before moving on: if you play as a duo, split the Beast between you. Since each of you can only use it once per round, decide who powers the perks and who smashes crates.
:::

## The rituals and the Pack-a-Punch

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/ruby-rabbit.webp" caption="Concept art of the Ruby Rabbit, one of the ritual sites."}

Each character has a **ritual site** tied to their story. At each one you must place a **personal item** that is hidden somewhere on the map and can only be reached with the Beast.

| Character | Ritual site |
| --- | --- |
| Nero (the Magician) | His apartment, above Easy Street, by the Junction |
| Floyd (the Boxer) | **The Anvil** boxing gym, in Waterfront |
| Jack (the Detective) | The **Ruby Rabbit** club, in the Canal District |
| Jessica (the Femme Fatale) | The **Black Lace Burlesque** club, in Footlight |

:::narration
To sum up the table: Nero performs his ritual in his apartment above Easy Street. Floyd at The Anvil gym on the docks. Jack at the Ruby Rabbit club in the canals. And Jessica at the Black Lace Burlesque in the theatre district.
:::

This is how a ritual works:

:::steps
1. Get the item (look for a crate only the Beast can reach or smash).
2. Bring it in human form, together with the **Summoning Key**, to the ritual altar and start it.
3. **Defend the area** until it finishes. Zombies and some ghostly figures show up.
4. When it ends you receive a **Gateworm**.
5. With all four Gateworms, head down to **the Rift** (the subway station under the city, with portals to every district) and find the **Sacred Place**. Place the worms and perform the final ritual: the Pack-a-Punch opens.
:::

:::callout{type="warn"}
Some rituals end with a very big visitor. Start each one with full ammo and an escape route in mind.
:::

:::spoiler[Where each item is]{level="quest" id="donde-esta-cada-objeto"}
- **The lawyer's pen (Nero):** in a crate hanging from a crane at the entrance to Easy Street. As the Beast, shock the electric meter so the crane swings round and drops the crate.
- **The championship belt (Floyd):** in a crate hanging from a chain above Waterfront. Grapple to the platform as the Beast and hit it to knock it down.
- **His partner's badge (Jack):** in a crate behind a locked grate in the canals. Find a brick wall with the Mark of the Beast, shock the meter hidden behind it to open the grate and smash the crate.
- **The producer's toupee (Jessica):** in a crate on the edge of a building, on the walkways leading into Footlight. Cross the gap as the Beast and knock it to the ground.
:::

:::spoiler[Who those items belong to]{level="lore" id="a-quien-pertenecen-esos-objetos"}
Each ritual sacrifices someone close to the character: Nero's **lawyer**, Jessica's **producer**, Floyd's **promoter** and Jack's **partner** on the beat. Their soul is stored in the Summoning Key and their body becomes the worm you receive.
:::

:::spoiler[What happens after the last ritual]{level="lore" id="que-pasa-al-terminar-el-ultimo-ritual"}

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/sacred-place.webp" caption="The Sacred Place, beneath the city."}

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/pack-a-punch.webp" caption="The Morg City Pack-a-Punch."}
The Shadowman drops the act: he thanks them, takes the Summoning Key and leaves. The sky turns red and an enormous tentacled creature appears floating over the city. The four have done exactly what he needed. The Pack-a-Punch, at least, is now open.
:::

## What to build

::::grid
:::card[Rocket Shield]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/rocket-shield.webp"}
Protects your back and can **ram** through zombies with three rocket charges. Find its three parts around the map and assemble it at a workbench. White gas canisters with a red valve refill the rockets.
:::

:::card[Civil Protector]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/civil-protector.webp"}
A **robot police officer** with a rifle that fights by your side for a while. First place three fuses (one per district, behind doors near the stations) in the fuse box under the city. Then call it for 2000 points at the emergency panels.
:::

:::card[Wonder weapon]{tag="Spoiler"}
The map has a weapon built from creature parts. What it is and where its parts come from is in the special weapons section, behind a spoiler.
:::
::::

:::callout{type="info"}
**Widow's Wine** is this map's new perk: when you get hit, you release a web that traps nearby zombies, and your grenades become web grenades.
:::

## Enemies and bosses

The usual zombies, in suits and hats, are the least of it. Morg City has new creatures. Each file is locked: open it the first time you run into one.

:::dossier[Morg City zombies]{kind="enemy" threat="1" open id="zombis-de-morg-city" img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/street.webp"}
The city's residents, back on their feet in period clothes. They work like on any map: they come in through the windows, get faster and tougher every round and go down faster with headshots.
:::

::::dossier[Margwa]{kind="boss" threat="4" id="margwa" codename="File 01" teaser="Shows up in the middle rounds, around round 8. If you hear something big, keep moving." img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/margwa.webp"}
A huge **three-headed** beast with tentacles, a creature of the Apothicons.

**How to kill it:** shoot the **heads only when their mouths are open and glowing**; the rest of the time they take no damage. You have to destroy all three. Each head gives 500 points and **releases a Parasite**, and the Margwa gets faster with every head it loses.

**Watch out for:** its **ground slam**, which downs you in one hit without Juggernog and breaks your shield instantly. Insta-Kills and Nukes do not affect it. It can vanish into a purple shadow and reappear where the other players are.

:::quote{by="Jack Vincent"}
You got my wife beat. She only has TWO faces!
:::
::::

::::dossier[Parasites]{kind="enemy" threat="2" id="parasitos" codename="File 02" teaser="Some rounds bring no zombies. Look up." img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/parasite.webp"}
Flying creatures that spit a liquid that smears your screen and hurts. They have their own **special round**, like the hellhounds on other maps: killing the last one drops a **Max Ammo**. They also come out of the Margwa's heads.

**Tip:** they are fragile but evasive. Put your back to a wall so they cannot surround you and fire in short bursts.

:::quote{by="Nero Blackstone"}
Once more, the Harpies come to tease and taunt us!
:::
::::

::::dossier[Insanity Elementals]{kind="enemy" threat="2" id="elementales-de-la-locura" codename="File 03" teaser="They fall from the sky, and not to say hello." img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/insanity-elemental.webp"}
Balls of twisted flesh with several screaming mouths. The community calls them **"Meatballs"**. They drop from the sky, roll towards a specific player and **explode on contact**.

They get their own special round after the second Parasite round, and later they sneak into normal rounds too. They are very fragile.

**Tip:** before one drops, a **red mist** appears where it is going to land. Shoot the mist and it dies before reaching the ground.

:::quote{by="Jessica Rose"}
Are those meteors? I don't think those are meteors.
:::
::::

:::dossier[The Keepers]{kind="enemy" threat="2" id="los-guardianes-keepers" codename="File 04" teaser="Ghostly figures that appear during the rituals." img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/keeper.webp"}
Hooded spirits that come at you while you perform the rituals. The Shadowman tells you they are the enemy: that they want to open the door for their master.

**The truth:** the Keepers are an ancient race that created the **Summoning Key** and have spent eons holding the Apothicons back. If they try to stop you, it is because the rituals serve the Shadowman's plan. Later in the story they become allies.
:::

:::dossier[The Shadowman]{kind="boss" threat="5" id="el-shadowman" codename="File 05" teaser="The voice that brought them here. You will catch glimpses of him around the city." img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/shadowman.webp"}
He presents himself as a guide seeking the four's redemption. In reality he is the **emissary of the Apothicons**, the tentacled creatures that want to devour entire dimensions.

Long ago he **was a Keeper**, but he was corrupted by the dark energy of the Aether and switched sides. He uses the four sinners to fill the Summoning Key with souls and clear the way for his masters. He is voiced by **Robert Picardo**.

**Trivia:** he lets himself be seen out of the corner of your eye around the city. Spot him five times in one match and you earn an achievement.
:::

## Special weapons

:::spoiler[The wonder weapon: Apothicon Servant]{level="weapon" id="el-arma-maravilla-apothicon-servant"}
::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/apothicon-servant.webp" caption="A living weapon that fires black holes."}

A **living** weapon that fires a purple **black hole**: it sucks in every nearby zombie and then explodes. When you reload it, you feed it.

It is built at a workbench with three parts:

1. **Margwa Heart:** dropped by the first Margwa you kill. If you do not pick it up in time, the next one drops it.
2. **Margwa Tentacle:** comes from the highest-tier **pods** (the purple ones), harvested with the Fumigator.
3. **Xenomatter:** from round 12 on, it can be dropped by Parasites or Insanity Elementals.

Once built, one player picks it up and it is also added to the Mystery Box. It cannot be upgraded with the Pack-a-Punch.
:::

:::spoiler[Li'l Arnie]{level="weapon" id="li-l-arnie"}
A tactical grenade from the box: a jar with a small tentacled creature inside. When thrown, it grows, lures nearby zombies and kills them; at the end it explodes. It only holds the Margwa in place.
:::

:::spoiler[The Apothicon Swords]{level="ee" id="las-espadas-apothicon"}
They are part of the secret quest. In short:

1. From the tram, look out of the red-lit windows: you will see three random symbols, one per trip.
2. In the Rift, next to the box and the GobbleGum machine, there is a wall with nine symbols only the Beast can see. Shock the three you saw.
3. Behind it is a statue holding eggs. Take one and charge it with souls at four Apothicon statues hidden inside smashable crates, one per district and another in the Rift (twelve kills per statue).
4. Return it to the sword statue. Every player has to charge their own egg.
5. To upgrade them, talk to your character's spirit at their ritual site, take the egg it gives you to four red circles on the map and survive the Margwas that appear.
:::

## The full story

:::spoiler[The secret quest: Apocalypse Averted]{level="ee" id="la-mision-secreta-apocalypse-averted"}

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/shadowman-key.webp" caption="The Shadowman and the Summoning Key."}
The map's main quest is called **Apocalypse Averted**. The "required" part is the rituals and the Pack-a-Punch. What follows is optional, long and hard:

1. **Swords.** Every player gets and upgrades their Apothicon Sword (see the special weapons section).
2. **The Keeper flag.** An open book in Nero's lair makes a flag fall. You have to carry it through each district, plant it on purple energy spots and defend it from waves of Insanity Elementals while the Shadowman attacks. Once per character.
3. **Capture the Shadowman.** The spirits become true Keepers, strip his shield and you have to gun him down until you drive him to the altar, where he is **trapped inside the Summoning Key**.
4. **The grand finale.** A giant worm appears over the centre of the city. Three players, as the Beast, electrify the tracks while the fourth calls the tram to run it over, and then they activate the Keepers in the centre.

This final step **only triggers with four players**. As a duo you can get as far as capturing the Shadowman.

**Reward:** the Civil Protector turns golden, with an upgraded rifle and faster revives.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending (cutscene)]{level="ee" id="el-final-la-cinematica"}
A beam shoots up from the centre of the city and destroys the giant creature in the sky. A Keeper descends with the Summoning Key to free the four from their curse… and right then a young **Edward Richtofen** suddenly appears. He grabs the Key, apologises ("I really don't have time to explain. I have a universe to set right") and leaves through a portal.

For the four in Morg City it ends badly: their dimension does not survive for long afterwards. For Richtofen it is only the beginning. You will see it in **The Giant**.
:::

:::spoiler[Small secrets]{level="ee" id="secretos-pequenos"}
- **Noir mode:** shoot the picture frame behind the ring at The Anvil and interact with it. The city turns black and white.
- **"Snakeskin Boots" song:** interact with three small wooden radios: upstairs in the boxing gym, at the entrance of the Ruby Rabbit and on a bench at the Footlight train station.
- **Skip rounds:** at the start, if everyone shoots the Shadowman peeking from the staircase to Nero's ritual room, the match jumps to round 5 (then 10 and 15) with bonus points. Only in private or solo matches.
- **Free points:** go prone in front of any perk machine and you get 100 points.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/cast.webp" caption="The map’s voices: Jeff Goldblum (Nero), Heather Graham (Jessica), Neal McDonough (Jack), Ron Perlman (Floyd) and Robert Picardo (the Shadowman)."}

- **Split the Beast.** Each of you has one use per round: one powers perks, the other smashes crates or acts as the reviving "taxi".
- **Remember the bottle colour** in the Junction before spending on doors.
- **Do the rituals together.** Defending an altar alone in the middle rounds is the fastest way to lose a match.
- **Careful with the tram**: it drops you off in the district, but it also leaves the horde waiting at the station.
- **If the Margwa shows up, do not run in different directions.** One of you distracts it while the other aims at the mouths.
