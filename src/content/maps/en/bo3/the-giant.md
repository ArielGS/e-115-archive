## Before you start

The Giant is a remake of **Der Riese**, one of the best-loved maps from *World at War* (2008). It is a **classic** map: no transformations, no rituals, no parts to build. Doors, box, perks, power and Pack-a-Punch.

That makes it **the best place to learn** if Shadows of Evil overwhelmed you: the map is small, easy to read and the main objective fits in one sentence.

:::narration
If you are coming from Morg City, breathe. Everything here is more straightforward. Factory, teleporters and dogs. Let's go.
:::

## Where you are and who you are

**Der Riese** ("The Giant") is a **Group 935** factory in Lower Silesia, near Breslau. Here scientists experimented with **teleporters** powered by Element 115. And here Dr. Richtofen locked his boss, Ludvig Maxis, and Maxis's daughter Samantha inside a teleporter: the exact moment the map begins.

The heroes are the **Primis**: younger versions of the four classic characters, the same ones who appeared in *Origins* (Black Ops II).

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/primis.webp" caption="The Primis: Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen."}

- **Tank Dempsey**: American spy.
- **Nikolai Belinski**: Russian spy.
- **Takeo Masaki**: Japanese spy. Fights with a katana.
- **Edward Richtofen**: Group 935 scientist, with a plan he shares with no one.

Something happens in the opening cutscene that leaves the other three speechless. If you skipped it or did not follow it:

:::spoiler[What happens in the intro]{level="lore" id="que-pasa-en-la-intro"}
It is the exact moment when the Richtofen of the old saga (the **Ultimis** one) has just locked Maxis and his daughter Samantha in the teleporter. The other three Primis, who had spent a long time following their own Richtofen's trail across dimensions, surround him and warn him not to open the teleporter door. He ignores them. Out of the light steps **another Richtofen, the Primis one**, who shoots his older self in the head without a word.

Dempsey sees him crouch over the body with something in his hand. In Der Eisendrache, Richtofen says that this is where he stored his other self's soul in an artifact, the Summoning Key. His only explanation for now: he does it "to secure a better tomorrow".
:::

:::spoiler[Why are there two Richtofens?]{level="lore" id="por-que-hay-dos-richtofen"}
The saga runs on **parallel dimensions**. The Ultimis (from World at War and Black Ops 1) and the Primis (from Origins and Black Ops III) are the same people in different dimensions. The young Richtofen is collecting the souls of the old versions, and to store them in the Key they first have to die. Why he does it is revealed over the following maps.
:::

## The factory

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/hero.webp" caption="Factory blueprint: the starting area, the courtyard and the three teleporters." wide}

- **Starting area (Mainframe):** where you spawn. It holds the central teleporter pad and the locked **Pack-a-Punch** door.
- **Animal Testing Lab** (to the left) and **Automobile Garage** (to the right): the two ways out of the starting area.
- **Courtyard:** in the middle. This is where the **power switch** is.
- **Teleporter A:** behind the Animal Testing Lab.
- **Teleporter B:** up the stairs from the Garage.
- **Teleporter C:** behind the Courtyard.
- **Bridge:** connects the Lab and the Garage up top. It only lowers once the power is on.

## The objective

:::steps
1. **Turn on the power.** The switch is in the Courtyard, behind the generator. It powers the perks and traps and lowers the bridge.
2. **Link the three teleporters.** Go to each one (A, B and C), activate it and **run back to the pad in the starting area** before time runs out. If you make it, it is linked.
3. **Open the Pack-a-Punch.** Each link opens the door a little more. With all three, the machine is free in the starting area. Upgrading a weapon costs 5000 points.
:::

:::callout{type="tip"}
Once the teleporters are linked, using one takes you back to the starting area. A perfect emergency exit when the horde corners you.
:::

## Your first rounds

:::steps
1. **Rounds 1 and 2 in the starting area.** Pistol and knife to save points. Rebuild the windows.
2. **Open towards the Lab or the Garage.** Either one; both lead to the Courtyard.
3. **Drop into the Courtyard and turn on the power** as soon as you have the points to get there.
4. **Find Juggernog.** On this map several perk machines spawn in different places each match, so explore. There are Juggernog, Quick Revive, Speed Cola, Double Tap and Mule Kick.
5. **Link the teleporters** between rounds, when only one zombie is left.
6. **Try your luck with the box.** This is where the **Wunderwaffe DG-2** shows up, one of the most legendary weapons in the series.
:::

## What to build

:::callout{type="info"}
**Nothing.** The Giant is a classic map: no parts, no workbenches. What it does have are **electric traps** in the same corridors as the original Der Riese: with the power on they cost 1000 points and electrocute any zombie that passes. Careful: if you walk through one without Juggernog, you go down.
:::

## Enemies and bosses

The Giant has no boss. The challenge is lasting through the rounds, and the special rounds are the most dangerous part.

:::dossier[Factory zombies]{kind="enemy" threat="1" open id="zombis-de-la-fabrica" img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/view-2.webp"}
The usual zombies, no surprises: faster and tougher every round.
:::

::::dossier[Hellhounds]{kind="enemy" threat="3" id="perros-del-infierno" codename="File 01" teaser="Every few rounds it suddenly goes dark, lightning strikes… and they are not zombies." img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/hellhound.webp"}
Zombie dogs wreathed in electricity or fire. They come in **special rounds** every four or five rounds: they appear with a flash of lightning, in packs, and head straight for you. The flaming ones **explode when they die**. Killing the last one drops a **Max Ammo**.

**Tip:** get into a corner or a narrow corridor so they can only come from one side, and back away a little from the burning ones before finishing them off.

**Their origin:** the first hellhound was **Fluffy**, Samantha Maxis's pet. Group 935's scientists put her in this very factory's teleporter as an animal test. What came out was no longer Fluffy. The others are her puppies.

:::quote{by="Demonic announcer, at the start of the round"}
Fetch me their souls!
:::
::::

## Special weapons

::::grid
:::card[Wunderwaffe DG-2]{img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/wunderwaffe.webp" tag="Box"}
An electric rifle that fires a bolt that **jumps from zombie to zombie**. Its magazine is tiny, but it clears whole corridors. It comes from the Mystery Box.
:::

:::card[Ray Gun and monkeys]{img="/images/basics/mystery-box.webp" tag="Box"}
The classic Ray Gun and the **Cymbal Monkeys**, grenades that lure zombies to them before exploding. Great for buying time.
:::

:::card[Pack-a-Punch]{img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/pack-a-punch.webp"}
Upgraded weapons on this map get a black camo with gold engravings, a tribute to the original Der Riese.
:::
::::

:::spoiler[The Annihilator]{level="weapon" id="el-annihilator"}
::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/annihilator.webp" caption="The Annihilator, waiting in the Garage furnace."}

A six-shot revolver that **disintegrates** zombies: a headshot kills in one hit up to round 24. It does not come from the box: it is the reward for the map's secret quest (the "Fly Trap"). Once you complete it, it appears in the Garage furnace and any player can take it as a special weapon, with 18 rounds.
:::

## Secrets and the full story

:::spoiler[The secret quest: the Fly Trap]{level="ee" id="la-mision-secreta-la-trampa-para-moscas"}
This map's quest is called **Paradoxical Prologue** and it is short. You can do it solo.

1. You need a **Pack-a-Punched weapon**.
2. Find the **hidden panel** in the alley between the starting area and the Animal Testing Lab, and shoot it with that weapon. Some objects fly off and hide around the factory.
3. Find and shoot these three:
   - A **Cymbal Monkey** inside the incinerator under Teleporter A.
   - A **teddy bear** on top of the tallest balcony, looking to the right from the power switch.
   - Another **teddy bear** inside the water well next to Teleporter B (the one on the left as you walk in).
4. When you finish, **Ludvig Maxis**'s voice says he has seen the signal: "I know where and when you are… Together we will ensure a better future for the children."
5. Pick up the **Annihilator** from the Garage furnace.
:::

:::spoiler[The secret perk]{level="ee" id="la-perk-secreta"}
With every teleporter linked and **Cymbal Monkeys** in your inventory:

1. Throw a monkey onto a teleporter pad and teleport along with it.
2. When you come back, one of the three lights on the panel next to the Mainframe turns from red to green. Repeat with all three teleporters.
3. Press the red button on the panel. The giant robot's head outside fires a laser that melts a pile of snow and reveals an extra machine: **Stamin-Up** or **Deadshot Daiquiri**.
:::

:::spoiler[The secret song]{level="ee" id="la-cancion-secreta"}
Find three **glowing green jars with spines inside** and hold the interact button on each one: two are in the Animal Testing Lab and the third is in a side room next to Teleporter B. A new version of **Beauty of Annihilation**, the original Der Riese theme, starts playing.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

- **To link teleporters, split the work:** one activates the teleporter, the other clears the way back to the starting area.
- **The bridge is a good choke point**, but not a good place to get surrounded.
- **Dog rounds:** together, back to back, in a corner. If you split up, the dogs split up too.
- **The Courtyard** is a wide area: a good spot to run a "train" in high rounds.
