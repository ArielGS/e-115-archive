## Before you start

Der Eisendrache ("The Iron Dragon") arrived in *Awakening*, the first downloadable map pack for Black Ops III, and for many people it is still one of the best. It is big (half again the size of The Giant), has lots of areas on different levels and brings back ideas from earlier maps: the Panzersoldat and Der Wunderfizz from Origins, launchers similar to the ones on Moon and dragon statues that eat zombies, like the Cerberus in Mob of the Dead.

Its main draw is **the bow**: you earn it by waking three dragons, and it can become four elemental versions, each with its own puzzle.

:::narration
This map is tougher than The Giant, but also more generous: almost everything you do gives you something back. Let's start with the castle.
:::

## Where you are and who you are

The **Primis** carry on with Richtofen's plan. This time they are chasing "a package": a truck of German soldiers climbing the mountains with a heavily guarded container. In the intro they are inside a **giant robot** chasing the truck, until a soldier brings it down with a rocket launcher. The four crawl out of the wreckage and have to continue on foot and by gondola to **Griffin Castle**, also known as the **Eagle's Nest**.

The castle is a Group 935 fortress with a **rocket launch platform** aimed at the Moon. Up there, at **Griffin Station**, is **Dr. Groph**, one of the last leaders of Group 935, who talks to Richtofen over the radio.

:::quote{by="Tank Dempsey"}
I should never have trusted you, Richtofen. Never.
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/approach.webp" caption="On the way to Griffin Castle."}

:::spoiler[What "the package" is]{level="lore" id="que-es-el-paquete"}
The container holds a **frozen Tank Dempsey**: not the Primis one, but the **Ultimis Dempsey**, the older version. The Germans keep him in cryogenic sleep as a test subject. Richtofen wants his soul for the Summoning Key, and young Dempsey does not yet know what that means.
:::

## The castle

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/castle.webp" caption="Concept art of Griffin Castle." wide}

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/view-2.webp" caption="One of the two cable cars in the starting room."}

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/undercroft.webp" caption="Concept art of the Undercroft."}

- **Gondolas:** there are two in the starting room. Zombies sometimes drop a **fuse**; use it to call a gondola, which arrives carrying a power-up or a weapon (and sometimes a zombie).
- **Courtyards (lower and upper):** the castle's central outdoor areas.
- **Bastion:** an outdoor area with the **Death Ray**, a huge trap.
- **Clock Tower** (Radio Room) and **church** (Mission Control): next to the upper courtyard.
- **Undercroft:** the castle's underground, with a **pyramid**, a teleporter and a workbench. It opens with the power.
- **Rocket platform:** outdoors. Watch out for the **engine tests** (see below).

**Getting around fast:** the **Wunderspheres** fling you through the air to another part of the castle for 500 points. You first have to activate each one's landing pad by hand.

:::callout{type="warn"}
**Rocket tests:** every so often an alarm sounds and the rocket fires its engines. The launch area fills with fire. If you are there, get inside before the countdown ends.
:::

## The objective

:::steps
1. **Turn on the power.** The switch is inside the castle, in the Armory, next to the Bowie Knife pillar. With power, the **Undercroft** opens.
2. **Assemble the Pack-a-Punch.** Its parts are spread across three places: the **Undercroft**, the **Bastion** and the **rocket platform**. Visit two of them and teleport the parts; the machine builds itself at the third.
3. **Wake the dragons** to get the bow.
4. **Upgrade the bow** into one of its four elemental versions.
:::

:::callout{type="tip"}
The Pack-a-Punch **moves** between those three places from time to time. Next to each one there is a board showing where it is right now.
:::

## Your first rounds

:::steps
1. **Rounds 1 and 2: save up** with pistol and knife, rebuilding windows.
2. **Head up to the courtyards** and open the way to the power.
3. **Turn on the power** and go down to the **Undercroft**: there is a workbench and one of the dragon statues.
4. **Start feeding the dragons** right away: you just kill zombies near them.
5. **Juggernog before round 10.** There are Juggernog, Quick Revive, Speed Cola, Double Tap, Stamin-Up and Mule Kick, plus **Der Wunderfizz** (1500 points), a machine that gives a random perk: it is the only way to get Deadshot Daiquiri, Electric Cherry and Widow's Wine.
6. **Assemble the Pack-a-Punch** by visiting two of its three locations.
:::

## The bow and the dragons

There are **three stone dragon heads** in the castle. Kill a zombie near one to wake it. From then on, every zombie you kill within its reach gets **eaten**: it lifts it into the air and swallows it. It eats one at a time, so do not rush.

1. In the **courtyard near Double Tap**, above the tunnel down to the lab.
2. In the **church** (Mission Control), where Speed Cola is, above the fireplace.
3. In the **Undercroft pyramid room**, across from the workbench.

When each one has eaten **eight zombies**, it turns to stone and crumbles. With all three done, the **Wrath of the Ancients** bow appears floating in the crypt right above the Undercroft, where a knight lies at rest.

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/bow.webp" caption="Wrath of the Ancients: bone, string and explosive arrows."}

**How to use it:** hold the trigger to charge the arrow for a bigger explosion. Its explosions **do not hurt you**, so you can shoot at your own feet if you get surrounded. If you bleed out, you can pick it up again in the same place.

### The four upgrades

Each elemental version is a different **chain of puzzles**.

::::grid
:::card[Storm]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/storm-bow.webp" tag="Lightning"}
Electric orbs that jump between zombies. The charged arrow creates a **vortex** that traps and fries everything it catches, the Panzersoldat included.
:::

:::card[Fire]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/fire-bow.webp" tag="Lava"}
A burst of lava. The charged arrow traps zombies in **volcanic mounds** that erupt a few seconds later. It gives a few extra points per kill.
:::

:::card[Void]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/void-bow.webp" tag="Demon"}
Opens a **portal** that releases skulls which hold zombies in place and devour them. You can walk through the trapped ones.
:::

:::card[Wolf]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/wolf-bow.webp" tag="Spirit"}
Slows zombies down. The charged arrow unleashes **spirit wolves** that tear through the horde.
:::
::::

:::spoiler[Where each upgrade starts]{level="quest" id="por-donde-empieza-cada-mejora"}
They all begin by shooting a hidden symbol with the normal bow, which drops a **broken arrow**:

- **Storm:** from the Bastion, looking towards the Clock Tower, there is a small tower with a lightning bolt painted on it and a weather vane on top. Shoot the weather vane.
- **Fire:** climb the spiral staircase in the Clock Tower. At the top there is a red symbol on the wall.
- **Void:** in the gate trap room next to Double Tap, the symbol is painted on the slanted ceiling between the two gates.
- **Wolf:** it starts by interacting with four paintings of the Wolf King around the castle, in a specific order.

From there, each path is a different puzzle built around the broken arrow.
:::

## What to build

::::grid
:::card[Rocket Shield]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/rocket-shield.webp"}
Back from Shadows of Evil with a castle look: it covers your back and rams with rockets. Three parts spread around the map.
:::

:::card[Ragnarok DG-4]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/ragnarok.webp"}
Two electric spikes: slam them into the ground to **kill everything around you** or leave them as a trap. They recharge as you kill zombies. Three parts, and one of them comes from a big enemy.
:::

:::card[Death Ray]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/death-ray.webp" tag="Trap"}
On the Bastion. For 1000 points it discharges electricity for a few seconds and kills everything that comes close. Careful: the cables on the ground hurt you too.
:::
::::

:::spoiler[Where the Ragnarok parts are]{level="quest" id="donde-estan-las-piezas-del-ragnarok"}
1. **The first Panzersoldat you kill drops it.** If you do not pick it up in time, the next one will.
2. **During a rocket test:** at the foot of the left staircase behind the rocket (the one closest to the Wundersphere) there is a lever with a red light. Pull it, run inside and hold out in the tunnel until all three lights on the console next to the teleporter are green and steady. Interact quickly: the part appears in the teleporter.
3. **After using the Death Ray for the first time**, a part appears floating above the Bastion. You grab it mid-air, launching yourself with a Wundersphere and pressing interact as you fly past it.
:::

## Enemies and bosses

:::dossier[Castle zombies]{kind="enemy" threat="1" open id="zombis-del-castillo" img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/view-0.webp"}
The usual zombies. During rocket tests and around the Panzersoldat, **flaming** or **electrified** versions show up, more dangerous up close.
:::

::::dossier[Panzersoldat]{kind="boss" threat="4" id="panzersoldat" codename="File 01" teaser="From round 12 on, something lands on a jetpack. You will hear it before you see it." img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/panzersoldat.webp"}
A zombie inside **armour** with a jetpack. It flies in from outside the map. It has a **flamethrower** and an **electric cannon** on its arm that stuns you and electrifies any zombie it hits. Its punch downs you in one hit without Juggernog.

**How to fight it:** aim for the **head**. If you destroy the **power core** on its back, it stops using the cannon. A charged bow shot (especially Storm or Fire) holds it in place for a few seconds.

**Reward:** the first one you kill drops a Ragnarok DG-4 part.
::::

:::dossier[Dr. Groph]{kind="character" threat="2" id="doctor-groph" codename="File 02" teaser="The voice on the radio. He is not in the castle." img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/groph.webp"}
A Group 935 scientist stationed at **Griffin Station**, on the Moon. He is waiting for "the package" to arrive and talks to Richtofen like an old colleague. They are not: Richtofen has come to **wipe out what is left of Group 935**, and Groph is next on the list.
:::

:::dossier[The Corrupted Keeper]{kind="boss" threat="5" id="el-guardian-corrupto" codename="File 03" teaser="The final boss of the secret quest. You will not run into it by accident." img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/corrupted-keeper.webp"}
It is the same **Keeper** that helps you during the secret quest. It travels to the Moon and brings back the **Moon Pyramid Device**, the machine Group 935 used to reach into the Aether. When you open it, the Keeper comes out **corrupted** by dark energy.

In the fight it uses the powers of the **four bows**: a rain of skulls, skeletons, mounds that erupt and an electric shockwave that downs anyone not in cover. Waves of **Panzersoldats** come with it.

**The key:** when a ball of electricity appears beneath it, plant the **Ragnarok DG-4** inside it and take cover behind a pillar. That pins it in place and exposes its weak spot, the **glowing rend in its chest**.
:::

## The full story

:::spoiler[The secret quest: My Brother's Keeper]{level="ee" id="la-mision-secreta-my-brother-s-keeper"}

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/boss-concept.webp" caption="Concept art of the final fight."}
The main quest is called **My Brother's Keeper** and it can be completed **with any number of players**, solo included. You need: one upgraded bow per player, the Ragnarok DG-4 built and the Death Ray used at least once.

1. **The beacons:** with the normal bow, shoot the electrified rods on the Undercroft teleporter.
2. **The sparks:** with the upgraded bow, shoot sparking objects around the castle (four, one at a time and in a hurry).
3. **Trip to the past:** the teleporter takes you back years to the lab. There you see Groph, pick up a canister and some fuses, and memorise the three symbols on his safe.
4. **Sabotage:** with the combination you open the safe in the present, power up the Death Ray and beat two memory games at the terminals. The Death Ray brings down a rocket carrying Dempsey's container.
5. **The Keeper:** after a second trip to the past, a Keeper spirit travels around the castle. You feed it souls by killing with the right bow inside four circles.
6. **The final fight:** the Keeper takes form, travels to the Moon and brings back the Moon Pyramid Device. When you open it, it comes out **corrupted** and the fight begins (see its file).
7. **The sky:** with the **Summoning Key** recovered, the terminal outside the tower launches the castle's rockets at the Moon.

**Reward:** every perk on the map.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending (cutscene)]{level="ee" id="el-final-la-cinematica"}
::figure{src="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/dempsey-capsule.webp" caption="The package, at last."}

The rockets destroy the **Moon**, and with it Groph and what was left of Group 935 at Griffin Station.

The four reach the capsule holding the old Dempsey, still alive. Richtofen uses the Key to freeze the others in mid-air and explains that this man "will one day wreak havoc across the entire universe" and that his soul can only be stored in the Key after death, "just as you saw me do to my other self".

As he is about to shut down the capsule, Dempsey stops him: if someone has to do it, it should be him. He walks up to the glass, looks at his older self and says: "Good to see ya, Tank." Then he switches it off. Richtofen puts a hand on his back to comfort him and then stores the soul in the Key.
:::

:::spoiler[Small secrets]{level="ee" id="secretos-pequenos"}
- **"Dead Again" song:** interact with three teddy bears: in Samantha's bedroom (on a chair), in the cell opposite the Juggernog cell (on a crate) and in the passenger seat of a truck next to the rocket platform.
- **Samantha's Lullaby:** there is a music box on the table next to her bed.
- **Disco:** in the Control Room, shoot the Moon globe to stop it and then the small rocket next to it.
- **Skeletons:** with the *In Plain Sight* GobbleGum active you can see three floating skulls (on the ledge near Double Tap, near Mule Kick and above the Control Room door in the upper courtyard). Shoot them with the bow and from then on, new zombies spawn as skeletons.
- **Free GobbleGum:** take the plant pot from the teleporter room and, when you travel to the past, put it back in its place. When you return, the plant will have grown.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

- **Split the dragons:** one stays in the courtyard by Double Tap and the other in the church. They fill up twice as fast.
- **Pick different upgrades:** a Storm bow and a Fire bow complement each other very well against the Panzersoldat.
- **Watch for the rocket alarm.** Many matches are lost by staying on the platform during a test.
- **The bow does not hurt you:** if you get surrounded, shoot the ground between the two of you.
