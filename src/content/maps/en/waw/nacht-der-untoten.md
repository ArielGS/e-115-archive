## Before you start

**Nacht der Untoten** ("Night of the Undead") is the **first Zombies map**. It shipped hidden inside *Call of Duty: World at War* (2008): it unlocked after finishing the campaign. Its name is a nod to George A. Romero's *Night of the Living Dead*.

It is a **tiny, bare-bones map**: three areas, three things to open and nothing to build. There is no power, no Pack-a-Punch and no secret quest. The original version does not even have perks: the only thing you can use is the **Mystery Box**. The challenge is pure: survive as many rounds as you can.

:::narration
If you are coming from the modern maps, this will feel like very little. That is exactly why it is so hard: nothing here will save you except your aim and the windows.
:::

:::callout{type="info"}
**Three versions.** This guide describes the original *World at War* map. The same map came back in *Black Ops* (Hardened and Prestige editions, and the Rezurrection pack) and in *Black Ops III* (*Zombies Chronicles*). What changes in each is in the section "What changes between versions".
:::

## Where you are and who you are

::figure{src="/images/maps/waw/nacht-der-untoten/crash.webp" caption="The Allied bomber that crashed next to the bunker."}

An **Allied plane crashes** into an abandoned airfield somewhere in German-occupied Europe. In the crash it wrecks some German army trucks that were carrying **the undead and Element 115** between **Group 935** facilities. The plane's survivors take shelter in a concrete bunker and hold out for as long as they can.

Those survivors are you: **four unnamed US Marines**. In this first version they do not talk, have no backstory and look different every match.

::figure{src="/images/maps/waw/nacht-der-untoten/marines.webp" caption="The Nacht der Untoten Marines, in the map's trailer."}

:::spoiler[Who were the Marines?]{level="lore" id="quienes-eran-los-marines"}
Nobody ever found out. According to the mode's official timeline, the four Marines hold out as long as they can and **eventually fall** to the horde. Years later, in *Black Ops III*, Doctor Monty sums it up in one of the map's radios: "You know what I just realized? You weren't even there. At least not at first. It was some other, random, forgettable soldiers that had the misfortune to live through that horrible night."

In the *Black Ops* and *Black Ops III* versions you play as **Ultimis** (Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen), who travel to this bunker later in the story; the wiki says the reason is unknown.
:::

## The bunker

::figure{src="/images/maps/waw/nacht-der-untoten/hero.webp" caption="The starting room, with the stairs to the upper floor and the “Help” door at the back." wide}

The map has **three areas**:

- **Starting room.** Large, on the ground floor, with **five windows**. Here you can buy two wall weapons: the **Kar98k** (200 points) and the **M1A1 Carbine** (600). It has two exits: the **"Help" door** and a **staircase blocked by a sofa** that leads upstairs.
- **"Help" room.** Opens for **1000 points** through the door with *Help* written on it: the P is only half painted, so it reads *Hell*. It has **two windows** and a wall leading to a dead-end cave, also boarded up. The **Mystery Box** is here, plus two weapons: the **Double-Barreled Shotgun** and the **Thompson** (1200 each). Its staircase, blocked by debris, leads upstairs for **1000 points**.
- **Upstairs.** The largest area, split into two rooms, each with its own staircase. It has **four windows**, some height in your favour and a lot of rubble in the way. Here you will find **Stielhandgranate grenades** (250), the **Sawed-Off shotgun** (1200), the **M1897 Trench Gun** (1500), the **BAR** (1800) and a **sniper cabinet** with a **scoped Kar98k** (1500).

::figure{src="/images/maps/waw/nacht-der-untoten/help-door.webp" caption="The “Help” door: the unfinished P turns it into “Hell”."}

:::narration
Quick summary of the bunker: the starting room downstairs, the Help room with the box to one side, and the larger upper floor, with the best wall weapons.
:::

## The objective

There is no quest to complete: **the objective is to survive** endless waves for as many rounds as possible.

:::steps
1. **Hold the starting room.** You start with an M1911 pistol, a knife and two grenades. Every board you repair on a window gives **10 points**, up to a limit per round.
2. **Open the map bit by bit.** The "Help" door (1000) leads to the box; the sofa on the stairs, to the upper floor.
3. **Try your luck with the box.** For 950 points it gives you a random weapon. This is where the **Ray Gun** comes from; it debuted on this map.
4. **Pick where to make your stand.** Once the map is open, decide which area to defend when the rounds get serious.
:::

:::callout{type="tip"}
**A tip straight from the wiki:** if you want to dig in upstairs, leave the **sofa barricade closed** (the one next to the grenades). That way the zombies have one less path to reach you.
:::

## Your first rounds

:::steps
1. **Rounds 1 and 2 with knife and pistol.** Zombies are weak at first: this is the moment to save points.
2. **Repair windows between waves.** On a map this small, every broken window is a new front.
3. **First useful buy:** the M1A1 Carbine in the starting room (600) keeps you going while you save up for doors.
4. **Open the "Help" room** when you can afford the door and a box spin.
5. **Do not run dry at the worst moment.** Wall weapons can be bought again to refill their ammo.
:::

## What to build

:::callout{type="info"}
**Nothing.** Nacht der Untoten has no parts, workbenches, traps or power. What you see when you spawn is all there is.
:::

## Enemies and bosses

No bosses and no special rounds: only zombies, faster and tougher every round.

:::dossier[Airfield zombies]{kind="enemy" threat="2" open id="zombis-del-aerodromo"}
They get in through the windows by tearing the boards off one by one; you can put them back (and get paid for it). Outside the bunker there are **explosive barrels** and some trucks that can be destroyed too.
:::

::::dossier[The cargo]{kind="enemy" threat="3" codename="File 01" teaser="The wrecked trucks outside were not empty." id="el-cargamento"}
According to the mode's official timeline, the German trucks wrecked by the plane **were carrying the undead and Element 115** between **Group 935** facilities.

It is the first clue of the whole saga: the dead did not rise by chance, someone was moving them from one lab to another. Who, and why, is told in the maps that follow and on the **Story** page.
::::

## Special weapons

::::grid
:::card[Ray Gun]{img="/images/maps/waw/nacht-der-untoten/ray-gun.webp" tag="Box"}
The most famous raygun in Zombies **debuted here**. It fires green rays with splash damage that hit several zombies at once. Careful: that damage hits you too if you fire too close.
:::

:::card[Mystery Box]{img="/images/maps/waw/nacht-der-untoten/mystery-box.webp" tag="Help room"}
It sits in the "Help" room and costs 950 points. In the original *World at War* version it was the only thing you could use on the map.
:::

## What changes between versions

**Black Ops (2010):**

- Cold War weapons in the box instead of World War II ones, plus two new wonder weapons: the **Thundergun** and the **Monkey Bombs**.
- **Mule Kick** (4000 points) in the starting room, added in a patch.
- The **Carpenter** power-up (repairs every window).
- You play as **Ultimis**, who speak with lines from other maps.

**Black Ops III (*Zombies Chronicles*):**

- Updated graphics and smarter zombies.
- *Black Ops III* weapons on the walls and in the box, alongside the Ray Gun, the **Ray Gun Mark II** and the Thundergun.
- A **Der Wunderfizz** (1500 points) upstairs, which gives random perks, and **two GobbleGum machines** (starting room and upstairs).
- The **Death Machine** power-up.
- You play as Ultimis, but this time they **do not speak**.

## Secrets and the full story

::figure{src="/images/maps/waw/nacht-der-untoten/ascend.webp" caption="“You must ascend from darkness”, on the wall of the starting room."}

The walls have messages written on them: *You must ascend from darkness* in the starting room and *Salvation lies above* in the "Help" room. The first line would come back years later as a step of the Origins quest.

:::spoiler[The radio in the "Help" room]{level="ee" id="la-radio-de-la-sala-help"}
To the **right of the Mystery Box** there is a radio. Knife it or shoot it and it plays music. What plays depends on the version: in *World at War*, tracks such as the Black Cats soundtrack, the Red Army theme or the *Königgrätzer Marsch*; in *Black Ops*, music from the campaign and *Dead Ops Arcade*; in *Black Ops III*, songs from the Zombies mode itself.
:::

:::spoiler[The secret song: Undone]{level="ee" id="la-cancion-secreta-undone"}
Only in the newer versions:

- **Black Ops:** shoot **three specific explosive barrels** visible from the starting room. **Undone**, by Kevin Sherwood, starts playing.
- **Black Ops III:** you have to destroy all **28 barrels** around the map.
:::

:::spoiler[Samantha's dolls (Black Ops III only)]{level="ee" id="las-munecas-de-samantha-solo-black-ops-iii"}
This secret plays **Samantha's Lullaby** and drops a **Max Ammo**.

1. Press the **four hidden buttons**: under the shelf at the bottom of the stairs in the box room; on the ceiling above the spiral stairwell going up from the starting room; on the wall to the left of Mule Kick; and on the wall across from the Argus upstairs. After the last one you hear thunder.
2. A **Samantha doll** appears on the floor in front of the RK5. Interact with it: a music box starts playing.
3. **Follow the music** to another spinning doll and shoot it until it disappears. Repeat until you hear the thunder again.
4. Go back to the first doll: a skeleton hand drags it underground, the Max Ammo drops and the song plays.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

- **Split the windows.** The starting room has five: each of you takes one side.
- **One buys, the other covers.** With so few points early on, decide who opens the "Help" door and who stays repairing.
- **The box is the only long-term way out.** Save points for spins instead of spending on wall weapons you will swap out later.
- **Do not split between floors.** If one goes up and the other stays down, neither gets there in time to revive the other.
