## Before you start

**TranZit** (also called *Green Run*) is the first Zombies map of *Call of Duty: Black Ops II* (2012) and, when it came out, **the largest in the saga**. It is not one enclosed area: it is **five stops** joined by a road, and a **bus** that drives around them in a loop.

It is a **complex** map. It brings lots of new mechanics at once:

- **Buildables.** You collect parts around the map and assemble them at workbenches: tools, traps, weapons and even the Pack-a-Punch.
- **Deadly fog** between stops, with creatures that attack you if you linger in it.
- **The bank and the fridge.** You can store points and one weapon for your next match (also in *Die Rise* and *Buried*).
- **Tombstone Soda**, a new perk to get your things back if you die playing in a group.

:::narration
TranZit is intimidating at first because it is huge. The key is to think in stops, not in one whole map. Let me walk you through them one by one.
:::

## Where you are and who you are

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/bus.webp" caption="The TranZit bus, your transport and your shelter."}

It is **2035**, ten years after the Earth was shattered at the end of *Moon*. You are on the outskirts of **Hanford**, in Washington State. The ground is cracked, **lava** seeps out of it and a **thick fog** covers almost everything.

The heroes are **Victis**, four survivors who met by chance:

:::card[Samuel J. Stuhlinger]{img="/images/characters/victis/stuhlinger.webp" href="https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Samuel_J._Stuhlinger"}
A former member of **The Flesh**, a cult that ate zombie meat. He hears a voice in his head… and he is not crazy.
:::

:::card[Abigail "Misty" Briarton]{img="/images/characters/victis/misty.webp" href="https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Abigail_%22Misty%22_Briarton"}
A tough, straight-talking cowgirl.
:::

:::card[Marlton Johnson]{img="/images/characters/victis/marlton.webp" href="https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Marlton_Johnson"}
A kind-hearted nerd, sole survivor of a nuclear incident at Camp Edward (the *Nuketown Zombies* map).
:::

:::card[Russman]{img="/images/characters/victis/russman.webp" href="https://callofduty.fandom.com/wiki/Russman"}
A former agent of **Broken Arrow**, a secret US research division. Years of exposure to Element 115 left his mind badly damaged.
:::
::::

They ride a bus driven by **T.E.D.D.**, a battered robot who greets the passengers… and gets angry if you shoot him.

:::spoiler[What happened to the world?]{level="lore" id="que-le-paso-al-mundo"}
In *Moon* (Black Ops), Richtofen swapped his soul with Samantha's and took control of the zombies. To stop him, his three companions helped **Maxis** fire **three rockets at the Earth** to cut its link with the Aether. The calculations were made in a hurry and the result was catastrophic: the planet was split apart and civilisation collapsed.

Since then **two voices** have been talking to survivors: **Richtofen**, who now commands the zombies from the Aether, and **Maxis**, now a bodiless artificial intelligence. Both want the same thing from you: to activate towers called **pylons** in their favour.
:::

## The map, stop by stop

The bus always starts at the **bus depot** and finishes in **town**, before starting over:

- **Bus depot** (start). Here are **Quick Revive**, the **M14** and the **Olympia**, plus the **three Turbine parts** and their workbench. At the start, a group of zombies stands still inside the station, waiting.
- **Tunnel.** Between the depot and the diner. No fog, but creatures at both ends. There is an **M16**.
- **Diner.** Small and tight. **Speed Cola**, an **MP5**, the **two shield parts** with their workbench and the **Mystery Box**, which always starts here.
- **Farm.** Also small: barn, shed and farmhouse. **Double Tap**, a **Remington 870**, **Claymores**, the **turret parts** and the kitchen **fridge**, where you store a weapon for another match.
- **Power station.** You go down through the door of an **outhouse** into an underground facility. This is where the **power switch** is built. It has **Tombstone Soda**, an **AK-74u** and the **electric trap** parts.
- **Town.** The largest area, with pools of lava. **Juggernog**, **Stamin-Up**, **Semtex** grenades, the **bank** (where points are stored and shared) and, in the bar, the **Jet Gun** workbench.

Off the route, only on foot and through the fog, there are more places: a **hunter's cabin** with the **Bowie Knife**, a bunker in the cornfields you will recognise if you played **Nacht der Untoten**, and a huge **electrical tower**.

## The bus and the fog

**The bus** is your transport, but you do not drive it: T.E.D.D. does, stopping at each stop for a while before moving on. If you stay inside with the doors closed, it may leave sooner. Zombies chase it, climb on and come in through the windows, which you can repair on the move. Inside, on the ceiling, you can get a **B23R**.

It takes three upgrades you find around the map: a **plow** that runs zombies over, a **hatch** and a **ladder** to reach the roof.

**The fog** covers everything between stops. If you walk through it, creatures burst out of the ground and leap onto your head (see the files below). **Lit streetlights** scare them off: they are lit by the Turbine or by the power.

:::callout{type="tip"}
**Portals.** If you lead one of those creatures to a **lit streetlight**, it digs a hole under it. Jump in and you will come out next to another random streetlight on the map. It is the fastest way to get around… if you do not mind where you end up.
:::

## The objective

:::steps
1. **Build the Turbine** at the depot. Its three parts (a mannequin, a fan and a tail) are right there. It powers doors, perks and streetlights near where you place it, but it drains over time and zombies can break it.
2. **Head out to the street and board the bus**, which always starts the match at the depot. The Turbine also opens a small room to the right of the lobby.
3. **Turn on the power.** At the **power station**, go in through the outhouse, gather the three switch parts around the facility, build it and flip it. From then on, watch out for storms (see the files).
4. **Open the bank vault** in town with two explosives: one for each armoured door.
5. **Open the bank basement.** The last door does not open from town: you have to place the **Turbine in front of the metal door at the bottom of the outhouse**, at the power station.
6. **Build the Pack-a-Punch** with the parts in the basement, at the far end of the hallway on the right. Upgrading costs 5000 points.
:::

::figure{src="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/outhouse.webp" caption="The power station outhouse: the way into the underground facility."}

## Your first rounds

:::steps
1. **Build the Turbine in round 1.** It is your key to almost everything on this map.
2. **Board the bus** when you are ready and get off at the **diner**: box, Speed Cola and the shield.
3. **Build the shield** as soon as you can. In TranZit it takes the same slot as the Turbine, so you will have to choose which one to carry.
4. **Chain the stops:** farm and power station. Turn on the power.
5. **Reach town** for Juggernog and the bank.
6. **Do not get left behind.** If the bus leaves without you, you will have to cross the fog on foot.
:::

## What to build

::::grid
:::card[Turbine]{img="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/turbine.webp" tag="Depot"}
A mannequin with a fan for a head. It powers doors, perks, streetlights, the electric trap and the turret. It also restarts the bus if it gets knocked out. It drains and blows up in the end.
:::

:::card[Shield]{img="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/shield.webp" tag="Diner"}
Made from a car door and a handcart. It protects your front if you hold it, or your back if you carry it, until it breaks.
:::

:::card[Electric trap]{img="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/electric-trap.webp" tag="Power"}
A portable barrier that electrocutes zombies. It needs the Turbine's power.
:::

:::card[Pack-a-Punch]{img="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/pack-a-punch.webp" tag="Bank"}
For the first time, you have to build it. Its parts are in the bank basement.
:::
::::

You can also build the **turret** (parts at the farm), the **power switch** and the map's wonder weapon, the **Jet Gun**.

## Enemies and bosses

:::dossier[Zombies of 2035]{kind="enemy" threat="2" open id="zombis-de-2035"}
The usual ones, with two twists: if they walk through **lava**, they catch fire and **explode when they die**, which can down you if you are close. They also chase the bus at full speed to climb aboard.
:::

:::dossier[Fog dwellers]{kind="enemy" threat="2" codename="File 01" teaser="You are not alone in the fog. Something shrieks beneath your feet." img="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/denizen.webp" id="moradores-de-la-niebla"}
**Denizens** (in-game, *Screechers*) burst out of the ground when you step into the fog. They shriek, run at you and **latch onto your head**: you cannot shoot, you cannot sprint, your vision blurs and they keep hurting you. If you do nothing, they down you in two or three scratches.

**How to shake them off:** five knife hits, or just two with the **Galvaknuckles** (on the diner roof). Before they leap they have very little health: one or two shots are enough. They give no points. According to the official timeline, they were created by **Broken Arrow**.
:::

::::dossier[Avogadro]{kind="boss" threat="4" codename="File 02" teaser="When you turn on the power, the storm begins." img="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/avogadro.webp" id="avogadro"}
A being made of **pure electricity** that first appears when the power is turned on, and afterwards at almost any stop (never in the tunnel). It arrives as a ball of lightning, takes a human shape and fires a bolt at you. It can also **knock out the bus** for a while.

**How to beat it:** normal weapons do nothing. Only **melee** hurts it while it is in human form (five knife hits, four with the Bowie, two with the Galvaknuckles), or **one EMP grenade**. Once beaten it flies off and comes back a few rounds later. If you wait for the storm to pass before killing the round's last zombie, it does not show up.

**Who it is:** a fragment of **Cornelius Pernell**, a former Broken Arrow scientist, transformed and driven mad.
::::

## Special weapons

::::grid
:::card[Jet Gun]{img="/images/maps/bo2/tranzit/jet-gun.webp" tag="Buildable"}
The *Thrustodyne Aeronautics Model 23*. It **sucks zombies** into its rotors and shreds them, even the ones behind you. It does not take a weapon slot, but it does take your buildable slot. Fire it non-stop and it **breaks**, and you have to rebuild it: watch the needle on the left and let it rest.
:::

:::card[EMP grenades]{tag="Box"}
They only exist in TranZit and come from the box. They shut down Turbines, perks, the bus and the Pack-a-Punch, stun zombies and **kill the Avogadro in one hit**.
:::
::::

**Jet Gun parts:** the engine is in the **tunnel**; the handbrake, in the **cornfield bunker**; the pressure gauge, in the **hunter's cabin**; and the wires, at the **power station**, after turning on the power. It is assembled at the workbench in the **town bar**.

## Secrets and the full story

:::spoiler[The two voices]{level="lore" id="las-dos-voces"}
**Maxis** says he wants to use the pylons to open a gateway to **Agartha**, defeat Richtofen and heal the Earth. In secret, what he wants is to **be reunited with his daughter Samantha**, even if that destroys the planet.

**Richtofen** wants the pylons to complete his control over the Aether and seal the rift to Agartha for good, damning Samantha. He can only talk to people who have **eaten zombie meat**: that is why only **Stuhlinger** hears him.

A television at the **farm** tells how other groups of survivors tried this before Victis, and how they sabotaged each other.
:::

:::spoiler[The main quest: Tower of Babble]{level="ee" id="la-mision-principal-tower-of-babble"}
It is done on Original difficulty and has **two paths**. Both start the same way: **turn on the power** at the power station.

**Maxis's path** (two players minimum):

1. At least two players build a **Turbine**.
2. Maxis asks you to **turn the power off**. Do it.
3. Get **EMP grenades** from the box.
4. Go to the **tower** in the cornfield (on the left side of the road, between the farm and the power station). Place **two Turbines** under it. When the **Avogadro** shows up right under the tower, kill it with an **EMP**.
5. Take the two Turbines to **two different streetlights** (for example, the depot one and the diner one) and place them at the same time.

**Richtofen's path** (only someone playing as **Stuhlinger** hears him):

1. Leave the power **on**.
2. Build the **Jet Gun** and fire it **under the tower until it breaks**.
3. Kill **25 zombies with explosives** under the tower (the Ray Gun and Ray Gun Mark II count).
4. Throw **four EMP grenades at four different streetlights**, which now crackle with electricity.

For the official story, the path that counts is **Maxis's**. It is the first of three pylons: the others are in *Die Rise* and *Buried*.
:::

:::spoiler[The secret song: Carrion]{level="ee" id="la-cancion-secreta-carrion"}
Find three **teddy bears** and press the interact button on each one:

1. Outside the starting area, on a **wooden bench** next to a pool of lava.
2. In the **farmhouse**, on a mattress on the second floor.
3. In the **town bar**, on a cushioned chair next to the pool table.

**Carrion**, sung by Clark S. Nova, starts playing.
:::

:::spoiler[Small secrets]{level="ee" id="pequenos-secretos"}
- If you look at the reflection in a **sniper scope** (like the DSR 50), you see the place **as it was before** the events of *Moon*.
- The **farm television** plays radio messages if you use it.
- You can **share points** with your teammates at the bank: deposit 1100 and a 1000-point reward drops that anyone can pick up. First you have to hit the drawer with the tags using the Galvaknuckles or the upgraded Ballistic Knife.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

- **Split the Turbine and the shield.** Since they share a slot, have each of you carry one.
- **Call it out before getting off the bus.** Splitting up in TranZit means crossing the fog alone.
- **One of you banks points** at the end of every good match: the next one will start easier.
- **When the Avogadro comes, one hits it and the other clears zombies.** With the normal knife it takes five hits: do not get distracted.
