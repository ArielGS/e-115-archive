## How it works

Every map with a guide has its **main quest** here (the big Easter egg that moves the story forward), turned into a list of steps you can **tick off as you play**.

- **Everything is locked behind the eye.** Only open the quest for the map you are about to play.
- **The instructions give hints, not exact solutions.** They tell you where to look and what to look for; you will find the details yourself. If you get stuck, each map's guide has more context.
- **Your progress is saved in this browser**, on this device, and it is the same in Spanish and English. Each list has its own **Reset** button.
- Maps without a main quest (the first ones in the saga) have their **best-known secret** instead.

:::callout{type="tip"}
Tick each step as soon as you finish it: some can only be retried the next round, and it is easy to lose count.
:::

## Nacht der Untoten (World at War)

It has no main quest. Its most complete secret is **Samantha's dolls**, which only exist in the *Black Ops III* version (*Zombies Chronicles*). Reward: a song and a Max Ammo.

::::spoiler[Samantha's dolls]{level="ee" id="las-munecas-de-samantha"}
:::checklist[Samantha's dolls]{id="nacht-dolls"}
1. **First button:** hidden under a shelf at the foot of the stairs in the box room.
2. **Second button:** look up at the ceiling, above the spiral stairs going up from the starting room.
3. **Third button:** on the wall, near Mule Kick.
4. **Fourth button:** upstairs, across from one of the wall weapons. Thunder will confirm all four are done.
5. **Wake the doll** that has appeared on the floor, in front of one of the wall weapons.
6. **Follow the music box** around the bunker and shoot each spinning doll until you hear the thunder again.
7. **Go back to the first doll** and let something from below take it.
:::

## Kino der Toten (Black Ops)

It has no main quest. The secret with the most story is **Maxis's film reels**, which tell what Group 935 was doing in this cinema.

::::spoiler[Maxis's films]{level="ee" id="las-peliculas-de-maxis"}
:::checklist[Maxis's films]{id="kino-reels"}
1. **Turn on the power** behind the stage and **link the teleporter** to the lobby.
2. **Travel to the Pack-a-Punch** and let the teleporter take you to one of its random rooms.
3. **Look for a reel** with the Group 935 logo. It can be in any of the four rooms: check tables, beds, carts and shelves.
4. **Teleport again carrying the reel** and load it into the projector.
5. **Second reel:** repeat the search on another trip.
6. **Third reel:** one more and Maxis's film is complete.
7. **Visit the conference room** one last time: on the table, something is ready for lift-off.
:::
::::

## TranZit (Black Ops II)

**Tower of Babble.** Two paths, one for each voice, on Original difficulty. **Maxis's** needs at least two players; **Richtofen's** can only be heard by whoever plays as **Stuhlinger**. For the official story, Maxis's is the one that counts.

::::spoiler[Tower of Babble: Maxis's path]{level="quest" id="tower-of-babble-el-camino-de-maxis"}
:::checklist[Maxis's path]{id="tranzit-maxis"}
1. **Turn on the power** at the power station: go down through the outhouse and build the switch from the parts in the facility.
2. **Build at least two Turbines** with the parts at the bus depot.
3. **Do what Maxis says** when he asks for something that sounds like a bad idea for the power.
4. **Get EMP grenades** from the box.
5. **Find the electrical tower** in the cornfield between the farm and the power station, and leave two Turbines under it.
6. **Wait for the storm.** When the electric being shows up right under the tower, finish it with something that only exists on this map.
7. **Take both Turbines to two different streetlights** on the map and place them at the same time.
:::
::::

::::spoiler[Tower of Babble: Richtofen's path]{level="quest" id="tower-of-babble-el-camino-de-richtofen"}
:::checklist[Richtofen's path]{id="tranzit-richtofen"}
1. **Turn on the power** and leave it on for the whole quest.
2. **Gather the Jet Gun parts:** one in the tunnel, one in the cornfield bunker, one in the hunter's cabin and the last at the power station, once the lights are on.
3. **Build it** at the workbench in the town bar.
4. **Push it under the tower** in the cornfield until it cannot take any more.
5. **Fill the tower with explosive souls:** 25 zombies killed with explosives under it (the Ray Guns count). Stuhlinger will let you know.
6. **Get EMP grenades** from the box.
7. **Knock out four streetlights** that are now sparking, one EMP each.
:::
::::

## Shadows of Evil (Black Ops III)

**Apocalypse Averted**, in two parts. The first opens the Pack-a-Punch and can be finished with any number of players. The second can be pushed forward with fewer, but **the final step needs four players**.

::::spoiler[Part one: the rituals]{level="quest" id="primera-parte-los-rituales"}
:::checklist[Apocalypse Ascendant]{id="soe-rituals"}
1. **Get the Summoning Key:** it is in the van in the alley where you start. The box only gives way to the Beast; you pick up the key as a human.
2. **The Magician's item:** hangs from a crane by the entrance to Easy Street. Something electric will make it move. His ritual is in the apartment above Easy Street.
3. **The Boxer's item:** a crate hangs from a chain above the waterfront. The Beast can reach it. His ritual is at the gym.
4. **The Detective's item:** behind a grate in the canals. Look for the Beast's mark painted on some brickwork. His ritual is at his nightclub.
5. **The Femme Fatale's item:** on the edge of a building, on the walkways leading to the theatre district. Her ritual is at her burlesque club.
6. **Go down to the Rift** with the four worms and find the Sacred Place. Each worm has its pedestal.
7. **Perform the last ritual.** The Pack-a-Punch is open… and the sky changes.
:::
::::

::::spoiler[Part two: the swords and the Shadowman]{level="quest" id="segunda-parte-las-espadas-y-el-shadowman"}
:::checklist[Apocalypse Averted]{id="soe-averted"}
1. **Ride the tram and look out of the windows:** on each trip you can see a symbol in a window lit in red. Write all three down.
2. **As the Beast, shock those three symbols** on the Rift wall next to the box and the GobbleGum machine.
3. **Take an egg** from the statue that appears and **charge it with souls** at the four Apothicon statues hidden in breakable crates (one per district and another in the Rift). Each player, their own.
4. **Return the egg** to the statue: your sword is waiting.
5. **Talk to the spirit** at your character's ritual site.
6. **Take its gift to the four red circles** near the ritual sites and survive what comes out of each one.
7. **Return to your ritual site** to upgrade the sword.
8. **Capture the flag:** it starts with a book in the Magician's apartment. Carry it through the purple spots, defend it and deliver it to each ritual site, once per character.
9. **Trap the Shadowman** in the Sacred Place: each player activates their Keeper, you break his shield and drag him to the summoning table.
10. **Avert the apocalypse (four players):** the giant worm in the city centre has a date with the tram. Three players as the Beast and one human.
:::
::::

## The Giant (Black Ops III)

**Paradoxical Prologue.** Short and possible solo. Reward: the Annihilator.

::::spoiler[The fly trap]{level="quest" id="la-trampa-para-moscas"}
:::checklist[Paradoxical Prologue]{id="giant-prologue"}
1. **Get a Pack-a-Punched weapon.**
2. **Find the hidden panel** in the alley between the starting area and the animal testing lab, and shoot it with that weapon. Three objects will fly off and hide.
3. **The monkey:** look under teleporter A. Something is burning down there.
4. **The first teddy bear:** look up from the power switch, at the highest balcony.
5. **The second teddy bear:** there is a water well near teleporter B.
6. **Listen to Maxis** and find your reward in the garage furnace.
:::
::::

## Der Eisendrache (Black Ops III)

**My Brother's Keeper.** Any number of players, solo included. It is long: set time aside.

::::spoiler[My Brother's Keeper]{level="quest" id="my-brother-s-keeper"}
:::checklist[My Brother's Keeper]{id="de-keeper"}
1. **Get ready:** one upgraded bow per player (solo, one is enough), the Ragnarok DG-4 built and the Death Ray used at least once.
2. **Go back to the original bow** for a moment and shoot the electrified rods at the top of the Undercroft teleporter.
3. **With the upgraded bow, hunt sparks:** four objects around the castle will spark, one after another. Phones, clocks, a radio, a globe… and little time.
4. **Travel to the past** through the teleporter. There are no weapons there: take the big blue canister, the small fuses, and memorise what Groph's safe shows.
5. **Back in the present:** a fuse into the Death Ray, switch its mode and open the safe from the terminal next to the clock tower.
6. **Big fuses into the coils**, the Death Ray back to destroy, and beat the **memory game** at both five-screen terminals.
7. **Press the green button** on the Death Ray. Something will fall from the sky: next to the wreckage there is an object you should not leave in the snow.
8. **Travel to the past again** and use what was in the safe on the computer next to the Kronorium. Take the stone tablet.
9. **Wake the Keeper** in the bow crypt and follow it around the castle: at each stop, kill with the bow of the colour you see. One of the stops needs the tablet.
10. **Open the MPD** with the blue canister from the past.
11. **The final fight:** each player leaves their Ragnarok on the blue zero-gravity tiles. In there, the Ragnarok is the key to exposing the boss's weak spot.
12. **Pick up the Summoning Key** and take it to the terminal next to the clock tower.
:::

## Blood of the Dead (Black Ops 4)

**Most Escape Alive.** For the ending, someone has to play as **Richtofen**. You need the Spectral Shield, the Hell's Retriever and the spoon.

::::spoiler[Most Escape Alive]{level="quest" id="most-escape-alive"}
:::checklist[Most Escape Alive]{id="botd-escape"}
1. **Spectral Shield, Hell's Retriever and spoon.** For the spoon: look for numbers on the pillars of the Warden's Office with spectral vision, enter them on the Citadel keypad, then see what hangs from the crane at the Docks.
2. **Release the Pack-a-Punch** from the Roof.
3. **Open the secret room** in the Warden's House: a scratched wall upstairs needs a very hard hit. Inside are a red stone and a body under a sheet.
4. **Take the stone** to the map in the lab and pick up the Kronorium… if they let you.
5. **Chase the seagull** around the island with spectral vision and hit it with a blast. You will have to find it several times, once per round.
6. **The last time you will only hear it crying.** A bit of zombie blood and the axe will do the rest.
7. **Put the Kronorium in the Warden's lap**, read what its pages show with spectral vision and take it to the keypad. The lighthouse will tell you where to go.
8. **The Docks trial:** some buoys blink in Morse code and a ghost needs an escort.
9. **The Showers trial:** a ghost lends you its banjo. Do not leave the circle.
10. **The New Industries trial:** a ghost that turns visible and a chain trap.
11. **The Powerhouse trial:** a memory game in Building 64, a punch card and some levers with symbols.
12. **The Michigan Avenue trial:** escort a ghost from the Cafeteria.
13. **With the five red orbs**, go back to the Warden's body. Survive what happens next and pick up what the Warden drops.
14. **Open the new door** in the lab. It is a point of no return: charged shield, upgraded weapons and every perk.
15. **The fight:** when the Warden glows red, destroy what floats around him and fire a blast at the top of the Dark Mechanism. Twice.
16. **Richtofen has to take a seat** in the Dark Mechanism. What comes next, you will find out yourself.
:::
