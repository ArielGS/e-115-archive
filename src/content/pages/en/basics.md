title: Survival manual
description: The bare minimum to know before your first Zombies match.
---

Zombies is a co-op mode for 1 to 4 players. There is no mandatory ending: you hold out against **rounds** that keep getting harder and, along the way, every map hides a story you only discover if you know where to look. These are the rules the game assumes you already know.

::::grid
:::card[Rounds]{img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/view-2.webp"}
Each round brings more zombies, faster and tougher. The red number in the corner is the current round. Between rounds you get a few seconds of calm: use them to buy things or move.
:::

:::card[Points]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/street.webp"}
Everything costs points: doors, weapons, upgrades. You earn them by landing shots, by killing (more for headshots and knife kills) and by **rebuilding the barriers** on windows.
:::

:::card[Doors and areas]{img="/images/maps/bo3/der-eisendrache/view-1.webp"}
You start in a small area. Opening doors and clearing debris costs points and unlocks the rest of the map. Opening everything early is not always smart: more areas means more windows for zombies to come through.
:::

:::card[Mystery Box]{img="/images/basics/mystery-box.webp"}
For 950 points it gives you a random weapon, sometimes a great one. If a teddy bear pops out, the box flies away to another spot on the map: look for the beam of light in the sky.
:::

:::card[Perk-a-Colas]{img="/images/basics/juggernog.webp"}
Soda machines that give you permanent abilities until you go down. The most important one is **Juggernog** (you survive more hits). **Quick Revive** lets you revive yourself if you play alone.
:::

:::card[Power]{img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/view-1.webp"}
On almost every map you have to "turn the power on" before perks, traps and machines work. Each map does it differently, and it is usually the first real objective.
:::

:::card[Pack-a-Punch]{img="/images/basics/pack-a-punch.webp"}
A machine that upgrades your weapon for 5000 points: more damage, more ammo and a ridiculous new name. Every map hides how to unlock it. In Black Ops III you can upgrade it again for 2500 to give it an elemental effect.
:::

:::card[Power-ups]{img="/images/maps/bo3/shadows-of-evil/gobblegum.webp"}
Some zombies drop glowing icons: **Max Ammo**, **Insta-Kill**, **Double Points**, **Nuke**, **Carpenter** (rebuilds windows) and **Fire Sale** (the box costs 10 points).
:::

:::card[Down is not dead]{img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/hero.webp"}
When you run out of health you fall and can still shoot your pistol. Your partner has a few seconds to revive you. If nobody gets there, you come back next round, but you lose your weapons and perks.
:::

:::card[Special rounds]{img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/hellhound.webp"}
Every so often a round changes: instead of zombies, hellhounds or other creatures show up, depending on the map. Killing the last one usually drops a Max Ammo.
:::

:::card[Wonder weapons]{img="/images/maps/bo3/the-giant/wunderwaffe.webp"}
Impossible weapons built with Element 115 technology: lightning that jumps between zombies, black holes, elemental bows… Some come from the box and others are built or earned.
:::

:::card[Easter eggs]{img="/images/basics/summoning-key.webp"}
Each map has a long secret quest that moves the story forward. It is optional and nothing tells you it exists. In this archive, all of them are behind spoilers.
:::
::::

## Element 115

:::quote{by="Group 935 archive"}
A mineral that fell from the sky, powers impossible machines… and raises the dead.
:::

It all starts with a meteorite. Inside it is an unknown element, **115** (also called Divinium). A German scientific organization, **Group 935**, discovers it can be used as an almost limitless power source and builds teleporters, weapons and other inventions with it. The problem: 115 also **brings the dead back to life**. That is where the zombies come from, and where almost the whole story of the saga begins.

:::callout{type="tip"}
**For your first match:** do not try to understand everything. Survive, open doors, get Juggernog and figure out how to turn on the power. The story pieces itself together map by map, and the rest is here whenever you want it.
:::

## Tips for playing as a duo

:::steps
1. **Stick together at the start.** Watch each other's backs in the starting area until round 3 or 4 and save your points.
2. **Rebuild windows between rounds.** Free points, and fewer zombies sneaking in.
3. **One opens, one covers.** Before spending on a door, decide who pays and who keeps watch.
4. **Run a "train".** In high rounds, running in a circle around an open area bunches the zombies up behind you so you can shoot calmly.
5. **Leave one slow zombie alive** at the end of a round when you need time to buy, build or explore without pressure.
6. **Juggernog first.** Before round 10, both of you.
:::
