## Before you start

Zombies maps tell their story in pieces: a hidden radio here, a character's line there, a cutscene if you finish the secret quest. This page puts those pieces together **in order**, from *World at War* (2008) to *Black Ops III* (2015). It is known as the **Aether Saga**.

What is out in the open is the context the game gives you without effort. Everything else (twists, deaths, endings) is **locked behind the eye**. Open it once you have played that map, or when you no longer care.

:::narration
One piece of advice before we start. If a name sounds strange, there is a glossary at the end. And if something does not add up, that is normal: the game tells it in pieces, and here we are going to put them in order.
:::

:::callout{type="info"}
**Play order and story order do not always match.** Time travel means that, for example, *Shangri-La* takes place in 1956 but is played after a map set in 2011. Here we follow the order in which the story **happens to the characters**.
:::

## Element 115 and Group 935

::figure{src="/images/story/meteor.webp" caption="An Element 115 meteor, in Shi No Numa."}

It all starts with a mineral that falls from the sky. In **1908** a meteor carrying **Element 115** crashes near the Tunguska River, in Siberia. Over the following decades more deposits turn up in Germany, Japan, the United States… and on the **Moon**. In the game it is also called **Divinium**.

In **1931**, after a large deposit is found near Breslau, Doctor **Ludvig Maxis** founds **Group 935**: an international, German-led research team that wants to use 115 to improve the human condition. Its base is the **Der Riese** factory. Maxis invites another doctor to join: **Edward Richtofen**.

With 115 they build **teleporters** and weapons like the **Ray Gun** and the **Wunderwaffe DG-2**, and they discover something terrible: the element **reanimates the dead**. To get more money, Maxis turns to the Nazi regime, which is interested in their weapons, their teleporters and those dead who come back.

::figure{src="/images/story/group-935.webp" caption="The Group 935 emblem."}

:::spoiler[Where 115 really comes from]{level="lore" id="de-donde-sale-de-verdad-el-115"}
Later games reveal it. In the beginning there were only the **Aether** and beings called **Keepers**. Some of them experimented with the **Dark Aether**, an energy "beneath" creation, and were corrupted: they became the **Apothicons**. After losing a war against the Keepers, the Apothicons were banished to the Dark Aether.

To get back, they **sent meteors carrying Element 115** to Earth across different dimensions, convinced that humanity would end up using it to wage war on itself and open a rift they could escape through. 115 is not a gift: it is **bait**.
:::

:::spoiler[Richtofen's trip to the Moon]{level="lore" id="el-viaje-de-richtofen-a-la-luna"}
In early **1940**, Richtofen and Doctor Schuster try teleporting a person for the first time, behind Maxis's back. Richtofen volunteers. He appears **on the Moon**, next to an ancient pyramid-shaped artifact (he will call it the **MPD**). While examining it he gets a shock and starts **hearing voices**, among them that of the being you will meet in *Black Ops III* as the **Shadowman**. He then jumps to **Shangri-La**, in the Himalayas, where the locals worship him like a god.

He returns to Der Riese weeks later, changed: obsessed with reaching **Agartha**, a place beyond reality. Other scientists unhappy with Maxis join his cause, and Richtofen has a base built on the Moon, **Griffin Station**. There they discover that the pyramid charges with **souls**: Richtofen starts sending soldiers and scientists to die next to it.
:::

## World at War: the origin (1945)

:::narration
First game. The year is 1945. And four maps that, at first, nobody knew were connected.
:::

**Nacht der Untoten.** An Allied plane crashes into an abandoned airfield. In the crash it wrecks some German trucks that were carrying **the undead and Element 115** between Group 935 facilities. Four unnamed Marines hold out in a bunker until they fall.

**Verrückt.** A squad of Marines led by **Tank Dempsey** reaches a Berlin asylum that Group 935 uses as a lab. Their mission: get **Peter McCain** out, an American spy who had infiltrated the group. They find the place overrun.

**Shi No Numa.** A Japanese facility in a swamp, next to a 115 meteor, handed over to Group 935. This is where the four classic heroes appear for the first time: **Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen**. Richtofen has come looking for something: **his diary**.

**Der Riese.** The four return to Group 935's main factory. Richtofen wants to use the teleporter to reach the Moon and confront **Samantha**, a little girl he knows from his past who now controls the dead.

::figure{src="/images/basics/ultimis.webp" caption="Ultimis: Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen."}

:::spoiler[The test subjects]{level="lore" id="los-sujetos-de-prueba"}
Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo are not Richtofen's allies: they are **his test subjects**. Nikolai was captured at Stalingrad in 1943; Takeo, on the Emperor's orders that same year; and Dempsey, at the Verrückt asylum, in September 1945. Richtofen experimented on all three until their minds were almost blank.

He did something else with them: using an artifact called the **Vril Vessel**, he put **a piece of the soul of all four** (his own included) into a rock of 115, which turned into a shard of glass. Without knowing it, he **bound their souls to the Aether across every dimension**. From then on, every version of these four, in any reality, is connected. This is the key to everything that follows.
:::

:::spoiler[13 October 1945]{level="ee" id="el-13-de-octubre-de-1945"}
At Der Riese, Maxis tests the teleporter with **Fluffy**, his daughter's dog. What comes back is no longer Fluffy: it is the first **Hellhound**. **Samantha** runs inside to get her, Maxis follows… and **Richtofen seals the door and switches the machine on**.

Maxis ends up in a strange place where he learns to **merge with electricity**. Samantha appears at **Griffin Station**, on the Moon, and climbs inside the **pyramid**, which corrupts her. Richtofen orders Maxis to be sent to the Moon to coax her out. Maxis persuades her to come out… and gives her an order: **"Kill them… all"**. Then he takes his own life and his mind merges with the base's machines. Samantha goes back into the pyramid and **unleashes the dead**.

The next day the outbreak hits Der Riese. Richtofen wakes his three test subjects, who remember neither who they are nor who he is, and they **agree to help him**. That is how **Ultimis** is born.
:::

::figure{src="/images/story/fluffy.webp" caption="Fluffy, Samantha's dog (in her Origins version)."}

:::spoiler[The diary and the spy]{level="lore" id="el-diario-y-el-espia"}
At Shi No Numa, Ultimis find the body of **Peter McCain**, the spy Dempsey had been looking for: he died parachuting into the facility. Richtofen recovers his **diary**, with all his notes on 115, and starts planning how to defeat Samantha.
:::

:::callout{type="tip"}
**Order matters.** The *World at War* maps came out in chronological order (June, September and October 1945), but the key moment at Der Riese, on 13 October, happens **before** Shi No Numa. That is why in Shi No Numa the four are already together and Samantha is already the voice announcing the rounds.
:::

## Black Ops: travels through time (1963-2025)

:::narration
Second game. The teleporter no longer just changes the place. Now it changes the era too.
:::

::figure{src="/images/story/wunderwaffe.webp" caption="The Wunderwaffe DG-2, the weapon that changed everything."}

At Der Riese, Richtofen tries to reach the Moon, but the **Wunderwaffe DG-2 overloads the teleporter** and throws Ultimis **eighteen years into the future**: to **Kino der Toten**, a Berlin cinema, in 1963. In the jump, Richtofen loses his diary. It is the first time they travel through time, and it will not be the last:

- **Kino der Toten** (Berlin, 1963): they find a **lunar lander** that takes them to their next destination.
- **Ascension** (Soviet cosmodrome, 1963): the scientist **Gersh** is trapped inside a machine and begs for their help.
- **"Five"** (the Pentagon, 1963): a separate map, with **Kennedy, McNamara, Castro and Nixon** as the heroes.
- **Call of the Dead** (Siberia, 2011): director **George A. Romero** is shooting a film with four real actors… and an outbreak hits.
- **Shangri-La** (Himalayas, 1956): two explorers trapped in a time loop help them get the **Focusing Stone**.
- **Moon** (the Moon, 2025): at last, **Griffin Station** and the pyramid.

Meanwhile, Samantha is still in command of the dead, and the mind of **Maxis**, trapped in the machines of the lunar base, is waiting for its chance to stop Richtofen.

:::spoiler[Richtofen's plan]{level="lore" id="el-plan-de-richtofen"}
Richtofen never wanted to just survive. Every map brings him closer to two objects: the **Vril Device**, which the actors of *Call of the Dead* recover for him, and the **Focusing Stone**, which they get in *Shangri-La*. With both he can **swap his soul with Samantha's** and take control of the Aether… and of the zombies.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending of Moon]{level="ee" id="el-final-de-moon"}
On the Moon, on 13 October 2025, Ultimis open the pyramid and **Samantha** appears, just as she was when she went in back in 1945. Richtofen merges the Vril Device with the Focusing Stone and **swaps his soul with hers**: Richtofen now controls the zombies from the Aether and Samantha is trapped in Richtofen's body.

Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo decide to help **Maxis** stop him. They fire **three rockets at the Earth** to cut its link with the Aether. But the calculations were made in a hurry: the planet is **shattered**. That is how *Black Ops II* begins.
:::

::figure{src="/images/story/samantha-moon.webp" caption="Samantha, inside the pyramid on the Moon."}

## Black Ops II: the broken world (2035)

:::narration
Third game. Ten years later. The Earth is broken, and two voices are fighting over what little is left.
:::

The Earth of **2035** is a cracked wasteland of lava and fog. Four survivors who met by chance, **Victis** (Stuhlinger, Misty, Marlton and Russman), travel on a bus driven by a robot. Two voices ask them for the same thing, to activate towers called **pylons**:

- **Maxis** says that with them he will open a way to Agartha to defeat Richtofen and **heal the Earth**.
- **Richtofen**, now master of the zombies, wants the pylons to **complete his power**. He can only talk to someone who has eaten zombie meat: Stuhlinger.

::::grid
:::card[Samuel J. Stuhlinger]{img="/images/characters/victis/stuhlinger.webp"}
Former member of a cult that ate zombie meat. The only one who hears Richtofen.
:::

:::card[Misty Briarton]{img="/images/characters/victis/misty.webp"}
Tough, straight-talking cowgirl.
:::

:::card[Marlton Johnson]{img="/images/characters/victis/marlton.webp"}
Kind-hearted nerd, survivor of Nuketown.
:::

:::card[Russman]{img="/images/characters/victis/russman.webp"}
Former Broken Arrow agent, his mind damaged by 115.
:::
::::

The pylons are in **TranZit** (United States), **Die Rise** (a ruined skyscraper in Shanghai) and **Buried** (an Old West town buried underground in Angola, dragged there by a rift in time). On each map, the main quest lets you choose whom to help.

*Black Ops II* also has two stories that seem unrelated:

- **Mob of the Dead**: four mobsters in **Alcatraz** prison, in the 1930s, try to escape in a handmade plane.
- **Origins**: northern France in **1918**, during the First World War, with **younger** versions of Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen.

:::spoiler[Who wins: Maxis or Richtofen]{level="ee" id="quien-gana-maxis-o-richtofen"}
There are two endings, and the official timeline says the one that counts is **Maxis's**.

**If you help Maxis** at all three pylons, he opens the gateway to Agartha. He travels to 2025, **pulls Samantha's soul** out of Richtofen's body and, as revenge, **traps Richtofen's soul inside a zombie** with blue eyes. He is reunited with his daughter in **the House**, in Agartha… but opening that gateway **destroys the Earth** and everyone on it. Samantha, seeing what her father has become, asks another Maxis for help, the one from **another dimension**.

**If you help Richtofen**, the rift is sealed, Samantha is damned to Agartha forever and Richtofen takes **Stuhlinger's** body to walk the Earth as he pleases.
:::

:::spoiler[Alcatraz and the loop]{level="lore" id="alcatraz-y-el-bucle"}
The *Mob of the Dead* mobsters (Sal DeLuca, Billy Handsome, Finn O'Leary and Albert "Weasel" Arlington) killed Weasel on the prison roof, and the other three were executed in the electric chair in 1934. But they are not in the real Alcatraz: the Warden, corrupted by the **Shadowman**, used their souls to create a **pocket dimension**, a trap that repeats over and over. At the end of the map you can **keep the cycle** or **break it**.
:::

:::spoiler[Origins: the other four]{level="lore" id="origins-los-otros-cuatro"}
*Origins* takes place in another reality, **Dimension 63**, on 4 June **1918**. There Group 935 is digging up ancient tombs full of 115 in northern France. The dead rise: they are **knights from a war fought centuries ago**.

The heroes are **Primis**: Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo, sent as **spies** by their governments, and a **young Richtofen** from Group 935. This Maxis has become obsessed with Samantha, who talks to him from another dimension: it is the call for help from the end of Buried. When 115 leaves Maxis catatonic, Richtofen **removes his brain** and puts it into a **drone**. The four free Samantha from her prison in Agartha, and Maxis's brain follows her.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending of Origins]{level="ee" id="el-final-de-origins"}
After the quest, we see a child's bedroom. **Samantha** and a boy called **Eddie** are playing with **action figures**: zombies like the ones from *Mob of the Dead* and *Buried*, a Richtofen figure and, under the bed, Marlton, Russman and Stuhlinger. Eddie complains she is doing it wrong ("I told you before, their eyes should be blue!"). As they leave the room, Eddie says: **"I wish the heroes in our stories were real, Sam."**

In *Black Ops III*, that room and those children appear again: it was not just a game, but the start of another part of the story.
:::

::figure{src="/images/basics/primis.webp" caption="Primis: the same four, in another dimension."}

## Black Ops III: the multiverse

:::narration
Fourth game. There is no longer just one world. There are many, each with its own version of the same heroes. And one of them has a plan.
:::

*Black Ops III* tells two stories that end up crossing:

- **Shadows of Evil** (Morg City, 1944): four strangers with shady pasts wake up with a mark on their hand. A man made of shadow, the **Shadowman**, promises them redemption if they perform some rituals. At the end **Richtofen** appears and takes the **Summoning Key**, an artifact able to manipulate the Aether.
- The **Primis** saga, with the Key in hand: **The Giant** (Der Riese, 1945), **Der Eisendrache** (a castle in Austria), **Zetsubou No Shima** (an island in the Pacific), **Gorod Krovi** (Stalingrad, with dragons) and **Revelations**, the finale.

The idea that explains it all: there are **many dimensions** with different versions of the same characters, and the Primis Richtofen jumps between them with a specific goal.

::figure{src="/images/basics/summoning-key.webp" caption="The Summoning Key."}

:::spoiler[The soul hunt]{level="lore" id="la-caza-de-almas"}
The young Richtofen wants to gather the **souls of Ultimis**, the older versions of the four, inside the Key. To keep them, those versions have to **die**:

- **The Giant:** he steps out of a teleporter right after the 1945 Richtofen locks Maxis and Samantha away, and **shoots his other self**. His soul goes into the Key. That shot **breaks space and time** into several fractured realities.
- **Der Eisendrache:** Ultimis Dempsey sleeps in a cryogenic pod. The young Dempsey, after hesitating, **switches off his life support himself**.
- **Zetsubou No Shima:** Ultimis Takeo is now a giant monster, tortured by Division 9 on the Emperor's orders. He accepts an honourable death: **he commits seppuku**, with the young Takeo assisting him.
- **Gorod Krovi:** Ultimis Nikolai survives alone in a Stalingrad full of dragons. After a fight, **the young Nikolai kills him**.
:::

:::spoiler[What the souls are for]{level="lore" id="para-que-sirven-las-almas"}
The souls travel to **the House**, in Agartha, home of **Doctor Monty**, a very ancient Keeper. There Monty turns them into **children**, to restore their innocence. Already there were Samantha, Fluffy, a boy called **Eddie** and a **Maxis** from Dimension 63.

Monty's plan: resolve the chaos of the multiverse and create **a new reality, free of Apothicons**. But it only works with the help of souls that **lived through and survived** the breaking of reality. In other words: Ultimis.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending of Revelations]{level="ee" id="el-final-de-revelations"}
Maxis hears the Shadowman's voice begging for help from inside the Key. He takes it… and ends up **trapped inside** while the Shadowman walks free. The Apothicons invade Agartha. Primis, with the help of **S.O.P.H.I.A.** (an artificial intelligence made from the brain of Maxis's assistant), defeat the Shadowman. Maxis, inside the Key, and S.O.P.H.I.A. plunge into the **Apothicon sun**: they destroy the Apothicons and **purge Element 115** from that final reality.

But Monty finds a problem. Primis should be fading out of existence, because they drank from **blood vials** from realities that no longer exist. They are a **paradox**. Richtofen suggests sending them somewhere no other version of them has ever been. Monty sends them to **1294**, to the **Great War** against the Apothicons… the same war whose knights rose in Origins. The ending closes a **loop**: *the cycle*.
:::

::figure{src="/images/story/the-house.webp" caption="The House, in Agartha."}

## Richtofen: the thread that ties it all

:::narration
If one character connects the whole saga, it is Edward Richtofen. But he is not just one man. There are several. And each one has his own file.
:::

Richtofen is in all four games: as a hero in *World at War*, *Black Ops* and *Black Ops III*, and as the voice ruling the zombies in *Black Ops II*. The trick is that **it is not always the same Richtofen**. Open his files in order.

:::dossier[Ultimis Richtofen]{kind="character" threat="5" codename="File R-1" teaser="The scientist who started it all." img="/images/story/richtofen-ultimis.webp" id="richtofen-de-los-ultimis"}
The original Richtofen. He joins Group 935 at Maxis's invitation (while secretly working for the **Illuminati**). In 1940 he touches the pyramid on the Moon and starts **hearing voices**: from then on all he wants is to reach Agartha.

- **1945:** he experiments on Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo, binds their souls to the Aether, locks Maxis and Samantha in the teleporter and forms Ultimis.
- **1963-2025 (Black Ops):** he leads Ultimis through time to the Moon, where he **swaps his soul with Samantha's** and takes over the zombies.
- **2035 (Black Ops II):** he is the voice speaking to Stuhlinger. In the official ending, Maxis strips him of his power and **traps his soul in a zombie**: **Undead Richtofen**, with blue eyes. His body is left in a coma.
- **Black Ops III:** in 1945, right after locking Maxis and Samantha away, he **dies at the hands of his younger self**, who keeps his soul in the Key.
:::

:::dossier[Primis Richtofen]{kind="character" threat="4" codename="File R-2" teaser="Younger, kinder… and with a plan of his own." img="/images/story/richtofen-primis.webp" id="richtofen-de-los-primis"}
The Richtofen of **Dimension 63**. In **1914** an older version of himself visits him and hands him two **blood vials**: "when the time comes, they will protect you".

- **1918 (Origins):** he saves Maxis's brain and helps free Samantha.
- **After Origins:** from the House, with Maxis, he prepares the plan: find the **Summoning Key** with the help of a book, the **Kronorium**. He discovers his connection with **Stuhlinger** and uses it to get Victis to fetch the book. In return, he rescues them from the doomed Earth and **freezes** them in his lab under Alcatraz.
- **Black Ops III:** he steals the Key in Morg City, brings the soul of an innocent child (**Eddie**) to the House, kills his other self at The Giant and **gathers the souls** of Ultimis.
- **Revelations:** his suggestion ends with Primis sent to the Great War.
:::

:::dossier[Eddie]{kind="character" threat="1" codename="File R-3" teaser="The boy playing with action figures in Samantha's room." id="eddie"}
He first appears at the end of **Origins**, playing with Samantha. *Black Ops III* reveals where he comes from: Primis Richtofen found **the soul of an innocent child** in another dimension, **2210**, and Monty gave him a body in the House. The wiki calls him **Edward "Eddie" Richtofen**.
:::

## Glossary

- **Element 115 (Divinium):** the mineral that powers Group 935's machines and raises the dead.
- **Group 935:** the research organisation founded by Maxis in 1931.
- **Aether / Agartha:** a dimension outside known reality. The wiki uses both names for the same thing. **The House** is there.
- **Ultimis, Victis, Primis:** the names of the three teams of heroes: the classic four (WaW and Black Ops), the 2035 survivors (Black Ops II) and the four from Dimension 63 (Origins and Black Ops III).
- **Dimension 63:** the reality of Origins and Primis.
- **MPD / the pyramid:** the artifact on the Moon where Samantha is trapped.
- **Summoning Key:** an artifact the Keepers created to manipulate the Aether. The heart of *Black Ops III*.

:::spoiler[Keepers, Apothicons and the cycle]{level="lore" id="guardianes-apothicons-y-el-ciclo"}
- **Keepers:** ancient beings who lived in the Aether and guard every reality.
- **Apothicons:** Keepers corrupted by the **Dark Aether**, turned into huge tentacled creatures. They want to return to Agartha.
- **Shadowman:** an Apothicon who travels between dimensions looking for a way to open the door for them. In *Shadows of Evil* he ends up locked inside the Key.
- **Doctor Monty:** a Keeper who lives in the House and pulls the strings of *Black Ops III*.
- **The cycle:** the idea that the story repeats. Revelations ends by sending Primis into the past, to the same war that already existed in Origins.
:::

## What the game never explained

There are gaps not even the wiki can close, because the game does not give the answer:

- **Why Ultimis return to Nacht der Untoten and Verrückt** in the *Black Ops* versions: the wiki says the reason is unknown.
- **Misty's and Marlton's past**: almost everything we know about them comes from their lines during matches, and it is still very little.
- **What happened to the Nacht Marines**: the official timeline only says they held out until they fell. No names, no story.

:::spoiler[What comes next: Black Ops 4]{level="ee" id="lo-que-viene-despues-black-ops-4"}
The Aether Saga **does not end in Revelations**: it closes in *Call of Duty: Black Ops 4*, which is outside this archive. There, Primis Richtofen realises that Monty has lied to him and that the Great War only closes the loop, and he decides to **break the cycle**.

In the final map, *Tag der Toten*, Primis Nikolai destroys the Summoning Key. Reality collapses and everything that came out of the Aether, zombies, Apothicons, Element 115 and Monty himself, is **banished to the Dark Aether**, Victis included. Only **Samantha and Eddie** walk towards the light, into a new universe: "a better tomorrow".
:::
