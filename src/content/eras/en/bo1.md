## What you need to know

Black Ops (2010) picks up the story that began in *World at War*. Its four heroes, known to fans as **Ultimis**, are three soldiers from enemy armies and a German scientist. They fight together because they have no choice: the dead have risen everywhere.

Behind it all is **Group 935**, which experimented with Element 115 at the *Der Riese* factory. There, Dr. **Richtofen** betrayed his boss, **Ludvig Maxis**, and locked him inside a teleporter together with his young daughter, **Samantha**. The girl ended up in a dimension called Agartha (or the Aether) and, from there, **she is the one controlling the zombies**. Hers is the childlike voice you hear in the background.

In this game the teleporters throw the group into different places and years: a Berlin cinema in 1963, a Soviet cosmodrome, a temple in the Himalayas… and finally the Moon.

## What changes in the gameplay

- **Classic maps.** Nothing to build: doors, box, perks, power and Pack-a-Punch.
- **New perks:** PhD Flopper (immune to explosions), Deadshot Daiquiri (better aim), Stamin-Up and Mule Kick (third weapon).
- **Wonder weapons** such as the Thundergun, the Winter's Howl or the V-R11.
- **Includes remakes** of the four *World at War* maps: Nacht der Untoten, Verrückt, Shi No Numa and Der Riese.

:::spoiler[Richtofen's plan]{level="lore" id="el-plan-de-richtofen"}
Richtofen never wanted to just survive. He hears voices promising him power if he reaches Agartha and takes Samantha's place. Across the maps he manipulates the other three into helping him, while Maxis tries to stop him from the shadows.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending of Moon]{level="ee" id="el-final-de-moon"}
On the Moon, Richtofen completes his plan: he swaps his soul with Samantha's and takes control of the zombies. Maxis answers with something desperate: he fires Group 935's missiles at Earth so Richtofen has no world left to rule. The Earth is left shattered, and that is how Black Ops II begins.
:::
