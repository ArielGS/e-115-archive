## What you need to know

Black Ops (2010) picks up the story that began in *World at War*. Its four heroes, known as **Ultimis**, are three soldiers from enemy armies and a German scientist. They fight together because they have no choice: the dead have risen everywhere.

Behind it all is **Group 935**, which experimented with Element 115 at the *Der Riese* factory. There, Dr. **Richtofen** betrayed his boss, **Ludvig Maxis**, and locked him inside a teleporter together with his young daughter, **Samantha**. The girl ended up trapped inside an ancient artifact, far from Earth, and from there **she is the one controlling the zombies**. Hers is the childlike voice announcing the rounds.

At the end of *World at War*, Richtofen tries to use the Der Riese teleporter to reach her, but the **Wunderwaffe DG-2 overloads it** and throws the group eighteen years into the future. From then on they jump between places and eras: a Berlin cinema in 1963, a Soviet cosmodrome, Siberia in 2011, a temple in the Himalayas in 1956… and finally the Moon in 2025.

## What changes in the gameplay

- **Classic maps.** Nothing to build: doors, box, perks, power and Pack-a-Punch.
- **New perks:** PhD Flopper (immune to explosions), Deadshot Daiquiri (better aim), Stamin-Up and Mule Kick (third weapon).
- **Wonder weapons** such as the Thundergun, the Winter's Howl or the V-R11.
- ***World at War* remakes:** the Hardened and Prestige editions (and later the Rezurrection pack) bring Nacht der Untoten, Verrückt, Shi No Numa and Der Riese.

:::spoiler[Richtofen's plan]{level="lore" id="el-plan-de-richtofen"}
Richtofen never wanted to just survive. Ever since he touched an artifact on the Moon in 1940, he has been hearing voices and is obsessed with reaching **Agartha**. His plan is to gather two objects (the Vril Device and the Focusing Stone) to **swap his soul with Samantha's** and take control of the zombies. The other three, with no memory, follow him. Maxis, whose mind is trapped in the machines of the lunar base, will try to stop him.
:::

:::spoiler[The ending of Moon]{level="ee" id="el-final-de-moon"}
On the Moon, Richtofen completes his plan: he swaps his soul with Samantha's and takes control of the zombies. Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo decide to help **Maxis** stop him and fire **three rockets at the Earth** to cut its link with the Aether. The calculations were made in a hurry: the Earth is left shattered, and that is how Black Ops II begins.
:::
