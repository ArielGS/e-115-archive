## What you need to know

*Call of Duty: Black Ops 4* (2018) tells **two stories** side by side:

- **Chaos**, a brand-new story: in 1912, the adventurer **Scarlett Rhodes** gathers three men her father trusted to get him back from **the Order**, a cult chasing ancient artifacts that can raise the dead. Its maps are *Voyage of Despair* (aboard the Titanic), *IX*, *Dead of the Night* and *Ancient Evil*.
- **Aether**, the **ending of the saga** that began in *World at War*: *Blood of the Dead* (Alcatraz), *Classified* (the Pentagon), *Alpha Omega* (Nuketown) and *Tag der Toten* (Siberia). Primis, Ultimis and even Victis come back.

If you have been following this archive, the second one is what matters: it resolves everything the ending of *Revelations* left open. The two stories never mix: you can play either one without knowing anything about the other.

## What changes in the gameplay

- **You choose the perks.** Before the match you pick **four**, and the map has one machine for each. Classics like **Juggernog**, **Speed Cola** and **Double Tap** are gone. In the Aether maps the machines are called *Brew*, *Cola*, *Soda* and *Tonic*; in the Chaos maps they are statues of gods (Danu, Ra, Zeus and Odin).
- **Elixirs instead of GobbleGum.** You bring up to four and trigger them with a button during the match.
- **Talismans.** One per class: it is used up at the start and its effect lasts the whole match.
- **Specialist weapons** that charge up as you kill zombies.
- **Zombie health cap** on Casual and Normal: from round 35 on they stop getting tougher.

:::spoiler[How the end of the Aether begins]{level="lore" id="como-empieza-el-final-del-eter"}
*Blood of the Dead* starts right after the cycle is broken: Primis reach Richtofen's lab under Alcatraz, another Richtofen hands them some blood vials and a **Kronorium whose pages have changed**, and the prison's Warden destroys their escape portal. They are stuck in the same trap that held the *Mob of the Dead* mobsters.
:::

:::spoiler[How the saga ends]{level="ee" id="como-termina-la-saga"}
After *Alpha Omega*, Samantha and Eddie join both crews of heroes. In *Tag der Toten*, Victis help build a device with which Primis Nikolai **destroys the Summoning Key**. Everything that came out of the Aether (zombies, Apothicons, Element 115 and Doctor Monty himself) is banished to the **Dark Aether**, and only Samantha and Eddie cross into a new universe. The long version is on the **Story** page.
:::
