## What you need to know

*Call of Duty: World at War* (2008) is where the Zombies mode was born. At first it was a secret: **Nacht der Untoten** unlocked after finishing the campaign and explained nothing: four unnamed Marines against endless hordes. In 2009 three more maps arrived in downloadable packs: **Verrückt**, **Shi No Numa** and **Der Riese**.

Behind it all is **Group 935**, an international, German-led research team studying **Element 115**. With it they built teleporters and impossible weapons… and found out that it also raises the dead. The other three maps are their facilities, and in every one of them something has gone wrong.

The heroes change along the way: in Nacht der Untoten they are four unnamed Marines; in Verrückt, **Tank Dempsey** shows up with his squad; and from **Shi No Numa** on you play as the four characters who will carry the saga for years (known as **Ultimis**).

## What changes in the gameplay

- **The essentials are born here.** Boarded windows, points for every zombie and the **Mystery Box**. In Nacht der Untoten, the box was the only thing you could use.
- **The Ray Gun** debuts in Nacht der Untoten.
- **Verrückt** adds the **Perk-a-Colas** and the **power switch**, which Nacht did not have.
- **Shi No Numa** brings the **Hellhounds** and the **Wunderwaffe DG-2**, a lightning rifle that jumps from zombie to zombie.
- **Der Riese** introduces the **teleporters**, the **Pack-a-Punch** and the **Monkey Bombs**.
- **Where to play them today:** all four came back in the Hardened and Prestige editions of *Black Ops* (and later in the Rezurrection pack). *Black Ops III* remade Nacht, Verrückt and Shi No Numa in *Zombies Chronicles*, and turned Der Riese into **The Giant**.

:::spoiler[Who the four really are]{level="lore" id="quienes-son-de-verdad-los-cuatro"}
Dempsey, Nikolai and Takeo did not join Group 935 as allies: they were Richtofen's **test subjects**. Dempsey was captured at Verrückt; Nikolai, at Stalingrad; Takeo, on the Emperor's orders. The experiments wiped out almost all of their memory. After the outbreak at Der Riese, Richtofen woke them up and, remembering neither who they were nor who he was, they agreed to help him. That is how Ultimis is born.
:::

:::spoiler[What happened at Der Riese]{level="ee" id="lo-que-paso-en-der-riese"}
On 13 October 1945, Maxis tested the teleporter with **Fluffy**, his daughter's dog. What came back was no longer Fluffy. **Samantha** ran inside to get her, Maxis went after her and **Richtofen sealed the door and switched the machine on**. Maxis ended up in a strange place where he learned to merge with electricity; Samantha appeared at a Group 935 base **on the Moon**, climbed into an ancient artifact, the Pyramid, and from inside it began to control the dead. That little girl is the voice you hear for years' worth of maps.
:::
